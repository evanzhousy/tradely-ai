# Tradely business strategy

Written 11 September 2026 from the live product, billing contract, course review, SEO notes, EASY Residency Season 5 brief, and public Reddit/X learning complaints. This is a working strategy, not a forecast. No customer, revenue, or win-rate claims are invented.

## Thesis

ChatGPT already teaches options vocabulary. Tradely is not a better textbook.

Tradely sells a checkable research path: a frozen case, a key the model cannot hold, a second case the learner has not seen, then one bounded task on live evidence in TradingFlow. The two products stay independent. The sequence is the offer.

If independent work stays “pick the cautious sentence,” ChatGPT wins and this is not a business.

## What we sell

**Tradely** (`tradely.ai`) is the classroom. Eight modules, 36 lessons. Synthetic cases, deterministic numeric and choice checks, saved attempts. AI coaching is a critic inside those cases, not an oracle. It is a built pilot, disabled by default, and must not be marketed as generally available.

**TradingFlow** is the live desk: recipes, ranking, flow, recap. Separate domain, account, billing, and data. Tradely Membership includes one month of TradingFlow membership, redeemed with the partner. Lifetime Course Pass is Tradely-only.

Do not market a merged product or a shared login. Partner copy already states this. Ads that imply one platform will break at checkout.

## The job, not the category

| Job | Who already wins | Tradely’s move |
|---|---|---|
| “What is a call / Greek / iron condor?” | OIC, YouTube, Claude | Do not compete. Free foundations can cover the floor. |
| “Pick a ticker / 0DTE setup” | ChatGPT, Discord, gurus | Refuse. That student is not the buyer. |
| “Write a backtest” | Codex | Out of scope. |
| “I was right on direction and still lost” | Nobody owns this as a product | Frozen IV/time/price cases. |
| “I paid for flow and it is noise” | UW/SpotGamma help docs, uneven | One print: open vs close, known vs inferred. |
| “This GEX wall — what am I allowed to claim?” | Conflicting Twitter threads | Units, sign, dealer assumption, then a second case. |

Social media (r/options, r/thetagang, X, 2024–2026) repeats the same pains: too much content, paid courses treated as scams, IV crush after earnings, flow as a firehose, GEX used as a magic level, paper trading that does not transfer, asking Reddit *after* the fill.

## Who we serve

**Beachhead (pay first):** people already spending $50–$200/month on Unusual Whales, SpotGamma, MenthorQ, or TradingFlow, who still cannot defend one print or one ranked row. They already believe the data matters. They lack a method that can be failed.

Warmest slice: current TradingFlow users. The lesson ends in their actual tool.

**Second:** English/Chinese learners on the same versioned cases. Interface chrome is bilingual; lesson bodies are still English with an explicit note. Do not claim full Chinese instruction until the bodies are reviewed translations.

**Not now:** absolute beginners whose only question is definitions; signal shoppers; quants who want an API; anyone who wants a profitable system or live P&L proof. We do not have that evidence and r/options will not believe it.

## Competitive stance

- **ChatGPT / Claude:** textbook and prompt-shopping. We win only on frozen cases, hidden keys, forced inspection, transfer, and a live-tool handoff. Never pitch “we have AI.” Pitch “the model is not allowed to certify you.”
- **Option Alpha / TastyLive / OIC:** vocabulary and strategy catalogs. We do not out-video them. We out-check them.
- **Unusual Whales / SpotGamma / MenthorQ:** data. We sit beside them, not on top of them. A UW user can finish Tradely without buying TradingFlow. Do not force the terminal.
- **TradingFlow:** partner, not a brand we absorb. Learn here, practice there, two accounts.

OpenAI’s ChatGPT for Financial Services (filings, citations, IB workflow) confirms the *constraint* thesis for a different buyer. It is not an options classroom. Do not panic; do not copy their pitch.

## Product strategy (next 90 days)

Priority is instructional honesty, not new surfaces.

1. **Independent cases must require the evidence.** The September 2026 content review found a first-answer shortcut and repeated guided questions in independent stages. Fix that before any growth spend. A learner should sometimes proceed and sometimes withhold, based on data.
2. **Ship a real packet, not sentence-picking.** Named inputs, a premium calc (`$2.05 × 100 × 500 = $102,500` is the canonical example), a claim, a limitation. Recap and audit that artifact.
3. **Keep the free floor.** Foundations (`option-contracts` → `expiration-settlement`) plus optional research previews (`audited-boundary`, `symbol-universe`, `rank-symbols`). Homepage starts at `option-contracts`. After lesson four, explain the next outcome and show Lifetime Course Pass before membership when that offer is on. Policy: `docs/free-course-strategy.md`.
4. **Public guides for GEX, OI vs volume, IV crush.** SEO research: those queries exist; the pages must answer the question anonymously, then invite a case. Do not paywall the definition.
5. **Coach stays a critic.** Two rounds, evidence-only context, no mastery write. Enable only after teaching-quality review. Marketing line if enabled: it cites the case; it cannot certify you.
6. **TradingFlow handoff stays one tool, one goal, one disclosure.** Deep link after the case, never as the first viewport for a cold beginner.

## Go-to-market

**Do not run “learn options with AI” ads.** That is the ChatGPT objection in the headline.

**Do run:**

1. **TradingFlow-in.** In-product and email to existing TF users: one ranked-row case, then the same task in Rank Symbols. Highest intent, lowest CAC if the partner allows it.
2. **Terminal-adjacent content.** Public worked examples that a SpotGamma/UW user would bookmark: net vs gross GEX, open vs close on one print, IV crush when the stock went your way. Cite units and assumptions. Invite the case, not a 36-lesson boast.
3. **Bilingual EN/ZH on the same cases.** Quiet channel. Do not overclaim translation coverage.
4. **SEO as specified.** Fix free-lesson promises, canonical host, sitemap, unique titles. Measure consented start → complete → checkout. No fake ROI.

**Do not:** Discord guru rooms, 0DTE signal channels, merged Tradely+TradingFlow brand campaigns, win-rate creative.

## Pricing and packaging

Keep the two offers. Do not invent a third until the free-floor conversion has a cohort.

- **Lifetime Course Pass — $49 once.** Current Evidence-Led Options Research and its revisions. Lead with this after foundations. Future distinct courses excluded.
- **Membership — $69/month.** All current Tradely learning while active, plus one month of TradingFlow membership fulfilled separately.

Say the second account out loud. If $49 does not convert people who finished four free lessons, the problem is the case quality or the paywall copy, not a missing SKU. Do not add more free lessons as an automatic response (`docs/free-course-strategy.md`).

Beachhead math for planning only, not a forecast: 1,000 members × $69 × 12 = $828k ARR. Option Alpha had ~85k unique visitors in July 2026; 1,000 is a small slice of a neighbor, not of Tradely users. We have no Tradely revenue baseline to cite.

## 90-day operating plan

**Days 1–30 — Make the exam real**

- Repair independent-case validity (position bias, true transfer cases).
- One complete packet + audit on a single capstone path.
- Confirm production free-floor copy matches access metadata.
- Ship or finish three public guides: GEX, OI vs volume, IV crush, each with one worked calc.

**Days 31–60 — Put the path in front of people who already have a terminal**

- TradingFlow practice links from the repaired cases.
- One partner or in-product prompt to TF users, if authorized.
- Watch consented: foundation start, case complete, pricing, verified course-pass access. Stripe remains payment truth.

**Days 61–90 — Decide**

- If people finish the free path and do not buy: fix the offer or the packet, not the ad account.
- If they bounce before finishing: the case is still too much lecture.
- If a small paid cohort repeats a second module: keep membership; do not pivot to signals.
- Coach: enable for a named allowlist only after human review of acceptance prompts.

EASY Residency, if admitted: spend the ten weeks on GTM with this beachhead, coach evaluation, and a Demo Day packet a reviewer can inspect — not a new brand merge.

## What success looks like (and what does not)

Use Stripe and the free-course measurement contract. Browser events are directional.

**Useful:** unique foundation starters → verified course-pass or membership, with sample size; repeat lesson activity among paid users; TradingFlow practice-link clicks that are not the whole story.

**Not useful:** lesson count, AI mentions, TAM slides, PostHog one-person funnels as MRR, “we compete with ChatGPT on explanations.”

## Kill conditions

Stop or rewrite the strategy if any of these stay true after the 90-day repair:

1. Independent cases still pass without reading the evidence.
2. The promised packet is still multiple-choice recognition.
3. Homepage or ads lead with AI or a combined Tradely+TradingFlow identity.
4. We claim outcomes, speed, or profitability we cannot show.

## One line

Tradely is the exam you cannot talk your way through. TradingFlow is the live desk for the same move. ChatGPT remains the textbook. Sell the exam to people who already bought the data and still cannot show their work.
