# S4E11 storyboard

1. **01 · Contract** — Start with the audit contract — Freeze the question, audience, selected session, source lens, and publish decision before reviewing the prose. — visual: `s4e11-contract`
2. **02 · Freshness** — Check freshness before polish — Confirm selected date, as-of timestamp, expiry scope, and source status; a polished stale value is still stale. — visual: `s4e11-freshness`
3. **03 · Lineage** — Trace the claim to the packet — Follow every number and chart label back to its Cookbook input, transformation, or explicit model boundary. — visual: `s4e11-lineage`
4. **04 · Chart** — Audit the chart, not just the headline — Axes, denominator, date, source, units, and lens must survive the trip from packet to recap. — visual: `s4e11-chart`
5. **05 · Missingness** — Keep gaps visible — Excluded rows, stale fields, and unresolved intent are part of the audit result—not cleanup work to hide. — visual: `s4e11-missing`
6. **06 · Challenge** — Run the challenge path — Ask what would lower the claim, rerun the recipe, or change the selected comparison before signing off. — visual: `s4e11-challenge`
7. **07 · Signoff** — Sign off with a boundary — Publish only what the packet supports, list what remains open, and assign the next review owner. — visual: `s4e11-signoff`
8. **08 · Practice** — Publish the workflow and review the season — Next: S4E12 · publish the reusable method, unresolved inventory, and next boundary for Season 4. — visual: `s4e11-outro`
