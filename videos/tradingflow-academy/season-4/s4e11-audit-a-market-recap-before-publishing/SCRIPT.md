# S4E11 narration outline

Audit a Market Recap Before Publishing

## 1. 01 · Contract

**Start with the audit contract**

Freeze the question, audience, selected session, source lens, and publish decision before reviewing the prose.

## 2. 02 · Freshness

**Check freshness before polish**

Confirm selected date, as-of timestamp, expiry scope, and source status; a polished stale value is still stale.

## 3. 03 · Lineage

**Trace the claim to the packet**

Follow every number and chart label back to its Cookbook input, transformation, or explicit model boundary.

## 4. 04 · Chart

**Audit the chart, not just the headline**

Axes, denominator, date, source, units, and lens must survive the trip from packet to recap.

## 5. 05 · Missingness

**Keep gaps visible**

Excluded rows, stale fields, and unresolved intent are part of the audit result—not cleanup work to hide.

## 6. 06 · Challenge

**Run the challenge path**

Ask what would lower the claim, rerun the recipe, or change the selected comparison before signing off.

## 7. 07 · Signoff

**Sign off with a boundary**

Publish only what the packet supports, list what remains open, and assign the next review owner.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Publish the workflow and review the season**
