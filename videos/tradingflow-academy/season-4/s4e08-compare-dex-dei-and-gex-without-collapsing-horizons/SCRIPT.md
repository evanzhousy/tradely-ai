# S4E08 narration outline

Compare DEX, DEI, and GEX Without Collapsing Horizons

## 1. 01 · Three lenses

**Start with three distinct questions**

DEX is signed directional impact. DEI is normalized magnitude. GEX is modeled structure context.

## 2. 02 · DEX

**Keep DEX signed**

The sign describes session-flow context; it is not dealer inventory or a directional instruction.

## 3. 03 · DEI

**Normalize before comparing**

Magnitude is comparable only when the denominator, source, and horizon are the same.

## 4. 04 · GEX

**Keep the model boundary**

Selected date, expiry, and prior-close assumptions frame a map; they do not forecast the next print.

## 5. 05 · Scope

**Align date and denominator**

Use the same symbol, session, expiry, source, lens, and denominator—or do not compare the values.

## 6. 06 · Conflict

**Let the lenses disagree**

Mixed lenses stay mixed. Do not average different horizons into a manufactured certainty.

## 7. 07 · Handoff

**Write what each lens supports**

Record sign, magnitude, model context, unknowns, and the next check without collapsing the horizons.
