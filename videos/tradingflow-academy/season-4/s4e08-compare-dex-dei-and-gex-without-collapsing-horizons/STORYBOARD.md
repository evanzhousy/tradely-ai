# S4E08 storyboard

1. **01 · Three lenses** — Start with three distinct questions — DEX is signed directional impact. DEI is normalized magnitude. GEX is modeled structure context. — visual: `s4e08-lenses`
2. **02 · DEX** — Keep DEX signed — The sign describes session-flow context; it is not dealer inventory or a directional instruction. — visual: `s4e08-dex`
3. **03 · DEI** — Normalize before comparing — Magnitude is comparable only when the denominator, source, and horizon are the same. — visual: `s4e08-dei`
4. **04 · GEX** — Keep the model boundary — Selected date, expiry, and prior-close assumptions frame a map; they do not forecast the next print. — visual: `s4e08-gex`
5. **05 · Scope** — Align date and denominator — Use the same symbol, session, expiry, source, lens, and denominator—or do not compare the values. — visual: `s4e08-scope`
6. **06 · Conflict** — Let the lenses disagree — Mixed lenses stay mixed. Do not average different horizons into a manufactured certainty. — visual: `s4e08-conflict`
7. **07 · Handoff** — Write what each lens supports — Record sign, magnitude, model context, unknowns, and the next check without collapsing the horizons. — visual: `s4e08-handoff`
8. **08 · Practice** — Open a research packet — Next: S4E09 · build a repeatable research packet in Cookbooks with the scope and boundaries attached. — visual: `s4e08-outro`
