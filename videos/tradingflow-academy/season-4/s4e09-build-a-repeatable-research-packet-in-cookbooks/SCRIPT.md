# S4E09 narration outline

Build a Repeatable Research Packet in Cookbooks

## 1. 01 · Question

**Start with one bounded question**

Write the symbol, session, lens, and output before opening a recipe; a packet should be challengeable.

## 2. 02 · Recipe

**Name the recipe fields**

Fix universe, filters, denominator, freshness line, and invalidation rule so the next run is comparable.

## 3. 03 · Inputs

**Attach inspectable inputs**

Keep the exact symbol set, contract scope, source lens, and selected date beside the recipe.

## 4. 04 · Ownership

**Make ownership explicit**

A reviewer challenges the read, a maintainer preserves lineage, and a runner records the next pass.

## 5. 05 · Run

**Run the packet without editing the question**

Cookbooks should make the same method easy to rerun; a new timestamp is not permission to change the boundary.

## 6. 06 · Record

**Record result and missingness**

Save observed fields, unknowns, exclusions, and the next check instead of filling gaps with confidence.

## 7. 07 · Challenge

**Leave a challenge path**

A packet is useful when another analyst can inspect the inputs, replay the run, and lower a claim cleanly.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Hand the packet to Market Recap**

Next: S4E10 · turn a completed packet into a grounded Market Recap with vetted charts and explicit caveats.
