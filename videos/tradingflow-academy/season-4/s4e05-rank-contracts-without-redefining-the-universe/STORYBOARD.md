# S4E05 storyboard

1. **01 · Two-stage** — Move from symbol to contract — Rank Symbols finds where to look; Rank · Contracts narrows the next inspection without changing the universe underneath. — visual: `s4e05-symbol`
2. **02 · Expiry** — Keep expiry in scope — A contract row is not comparable until expiry, session, and as-of freshness remain explicit. — visual: `s4e05-expiry`
3. **03 · Moneyness** — Read strike and moneyness together — Strike, spot reference, and moneyness describe the contract; none supplies direction by itself. — visual: `s4e05-moneyness`
4. **04 · Neighborhood** — Use the contract neighborhood — Keep nearby strikes, paired puts or calls, and DTE visible before promoting one row. — visual: `s4e05-neighborhood`
5. **05 · Repetition** — Treat repetition as evidence to inspect — Repeated prints can motivate a check; they do not prove intent, accumulation, or a future move. — visual: `s4e05-repetition`
6. **06 · Compare** — Compare contracts like with like — Same symbol, expiry, session, lens, and freshness line—then record the difference you actually observed. — visual: `s4e05-compare`
7. **07 · Boundary** — Keep contract rank descriptive — Rank narrows the queue; it does not become a strategy, probability, dealer position, or trade decision. — visual: `s4e05-boundary`
8. **08 · Practice** — Validate one print in Option Trades — Next: S4E06 · validate one print with bid/ask location, premium, size, repetition, and quote context. — visual: `s4e05-outro`
