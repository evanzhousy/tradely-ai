# S4E05 narration outline

Rank Contracts Without Redefining the Universe

## 1. 01 · Two-stage

**Move from symbol to contract**

Rank Symbols finds where to look; Rank · Contracts narrows the next inspection without changing the universe underneath.

## 2. 02 · Expiry

**Keep expiry in scope**

A contract row is not comparable until expiry, session, and as-of freshness remain explicit.

## 3. 03 · Moneyness

**Read strike and moneyness together**

Strike, spot reference, and moneyness describe the contract; none supplies direction by itself.

## 4. 04 · Neighborhood

**Use the contract neighborhood**

Keep nearby strikes, paired puts or calls, and DTE visible before promoting one row.

## 5. 05 · Repetition

**Treat repetition as evidence to inspect**

Repeated prints can motivate a check; they do not prove intent, accumulation, or a future move.

## 6. 06 · Compare

**Compare contracts like with like**

Same symbol, expiry, session, lens, and freshness line—then record the difference you actually observed.

## 7. 07 · Boundary

**Keep contract rank descriptive**

Rank narrows the queue; it does not become a strategy, probability, dealer position, or trade decision.
