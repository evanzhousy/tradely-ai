# S4E01 storyboard

1. **01 · Handoff** — Carry the audited boundary — Start with the Season 3 audit: preserve history, inherit fields, and keep the next question separate from prior conclusions. — visual: `s4e01-handoff`
2. **02 · Question** — Name one research question — Ask a bounded question about one session, one source lens, and one decision—not a promise about direction. — visual: `s4e01-question`
3. **03 · Scope** — Declare the source and horizon — Record symbol universe, session window, freshness, contract scope, and the data lens before opening a candidate. — visual: `s4e01-scope`
4. **04 · Required** — Lock the required fields — Identity, sequence, source, freshness, and invalidation are intake requirements, not optional annotations. — visual: `s4e01-required`
5. **05 · Owner** — Assign the research owner — Name the reviewer, packet maintainer, replay operator, and escalation path before the first row enters. — visual: `s4e01-owner`
6. **06 · Packet** — Open a controlled packet — Keep observed, inferred, contradicted, and unknown states separate so the next workflow remains challengeable. — visual: `s4e01-packet`
7. **07 · Boundary** — Freeze the next test — Future rows can test the question; the audited Season 3 history remains published and unchanged. — visual: `s4e01-boundary`
8. **08 · Practice** — Choose the symbol universe — Next: S4E02 · choose the correct symbol universe before Rank · Symbols concentrates attention. — visual: `s4e01-outro`
