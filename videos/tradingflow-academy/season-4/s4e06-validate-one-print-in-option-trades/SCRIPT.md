# S4E06 narration outline

Validate One Print in Option Trades

## 1. 01 · Print

**Start with one observed print**

Open Option Trades and freeze the exact symbol, contract, timestamp, side, size, and session before interpretation.

## 2. 02 · Location

**Read bid and ask location**

Ask whether the execution is at bid, mid, or ask; location describes the print, not the trader's intent.

## 3. 03 · Premium

**Keep premium and size separate**

Premium is price per contract; size is quantity. Both need the same contract and timestamp context.

## 4. 04 · Context

**Return to the contract drawer**

Confirm expiry, strike, moneyness, DTE, and source freshness still match the print you opened.

## 5. 05 · Repetition

**Check repetition without inferring accumulation**

A repeated contract can justify a follow-up query; it cannot establish opening, closing, or accumulation on its own.

## 6. 06 · Quote

**Attach quote context**

Record NBBO, neighboring prints, timing, and the next snapshot before treating a print as a research candidate.

## 7. 07 · Boundary

**Leave unresolved claims open**

Observed execution is not complete intent. Separate fact, unknown, and next check in the handoff.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Separate session flow from structure**

Next: S4E07 · keep today's tape separate from open interest and reported standing context.
