# S4E12 storyboard

1. **01 · Method** — Publish the method, not a promise — Discover → Inspect → Validate → Compare → Decide is a repeatable research loop, not a directional shortcut. — visual: `s4e12-method`
2. **02 · Packet** — Keep the packet reusable — Question, scope, inputs, recipe, owner, freshness, and next check travel together into the next run. — visual: `s4e12-packet`
3. **03 · Unresolved** — Publish the unresolved inventory — Missing fields, stale context, untested alternatives, and unknown intent remain part of the method review. — visual: `s4e12-unresolved`
4. **04 · Cadence** — Assign the review cadence — A maintainer preserves lineage, a reviewer challenges the read, and a runner records what changed. — visual: `s4e12-cadence`
5. **05 · Boundary** — Set the publish boundary — State what the season supports and what it cannot say about direction, intent, probability, or outcomes. — visual: `s4e12-publish`
6. **06 · Carry** — Carry evidence across episodes — The next cycle inherits the packet and vocabulary without rewriting historical observations. — visual: `s4e12-carry`
7. **07 · Next** — Name the next research boundary — Choose a new question, keep the old history available, and make the next repair challengeable from day one. — visual: `s4e12-next`
8. **08 · Practice** — Season 4 complete — Carry the reusable packet, unresolved inventory, and explicit boundary into the next research cycle. — visual: `s4e12-outro`
