# S4E12 narration outline

Publish the Workflow and Review the Season

## 1. 01 · Method

**Publish the method, not a promise**

Discover → Inspect → Validate → Compare → Decide is a repeatable research loop, not a directional shortcut.

## 2. 02 · Packet

**Keep the packet reusable**

Question, scope, inputs, recipe, owner, freshness, and next check travel together into the next run.

## 3. 03 · Unresolved

**Publish the unresolved inventory**

Missing fields, stale context, untested alternatives, and unknown intent remain part of the method review.

## 4. 04 · Cadence

**Assign the review cadence**

A maintainer preserves lineage, a reviewer challenges the read, and a runner records what changed.

## 5. 05 · Boundary

**Set the publish boundary**

State what the season supports and what it cannot say about direction, intent, probability, or outcomes.

## 6. 06 · Carry

**Carry evidence across episodes**

The next cycle inherits the packet and vocabulary without rewriting historical observations.

## 7. 07 · Next

**Name the next research boundary**

Choose a new question, keep the old history available, and make the next repair challengeable from day one.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Season 4 complete**

Carry the reusable packet, unresolved inventory, and explicit boundary into the next research cycle.

## Practice

Season 4 complete · carry the method into the next research cycle.
