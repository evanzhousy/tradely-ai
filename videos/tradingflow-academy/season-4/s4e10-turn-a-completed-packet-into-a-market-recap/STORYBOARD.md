# S4E10 storyboard

1. **01 · Packet** — Start from a completed packet — Do not draft from memory; carry the recipe, selected session, source lens, and unresolved inventory into the recap. — visual: `s4e10-packet`
2. **02 · Evidence** — Choose evidence with lineage — Every number in the recap should point back to an observed field, a named model, or an explicit unknown. — visual: `s4e10-evidence`
3. **03 · Chart** — Use a chart that answers the question — A chart earns its place when axes, date, denominator, and source make the comparison inspectable. — visual: `s4e10-chart`
4. **04 · Headline** — Write the headline at the right altitude — Describe what the packet supports; do not turn rank, DEX, DEI, or GEX into a forecast headline. — visual: `s4e10-headline`
5. **05 · Caveats** — Keep caveats beside the number — Freshness, missingness, model assumptions, and unresolved intent belong next to the claim—not in a footnote nobody reads. — visual: `s4e10-caveats`
6. **06 · Compare** — Compare lenses without collapsing them — A recap can show signed flow, normalized magnitude, and modeled structure together while keeping their horizons separate. — visual: `s4e10-compare`
7. **07 · Ready** — Check the publish boundary — Confirm lineage, chart labels, scope line, caveats, and challenge path before calling the recap ready. — visual: `s4e10-ready`
8. **08 · Practice** — Hand it to the audit — Next: S4E11 · audit a Market Recap for freshness, lineage, missingness, and a real challenge path. — visual: `s4e10-outro`
