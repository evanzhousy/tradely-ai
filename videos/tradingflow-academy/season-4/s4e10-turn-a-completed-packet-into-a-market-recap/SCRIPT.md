# S4E10 narration outline

Turn a Completed Packet into a Market Recap

## 1. 01 · Packet

**Start from a completed packet**

Do not draft from memory; carry the recipe, selected session, source lens, and unresolved inventory into the recap.

## 2. 02 · Evidence

**Choose evidence with lineage**

Every number in the recap should point back to an observed field, a named model, or an explicit unknown.

## 3. 03 · Chart

**Use a chart that answers the question**

A chart earns its place when axes, date, denominator, and source make the comparison inspectable.

## 4. 04 · Headline

**Write the headline at the right altitude**

Describe what the packet supports; do not turn rank, DEX, DEI, or GEX into a forecast headline.

## 5. 05 · Caveats

**Keep caveats beside the number**

Freshness, missingness, model assumptions, and unresolved intent belong next to the claim—not in a footnote nobody reads.

## 6. 06 · Compare

**Compare lenses without collapsing them**

A recap can show signed flow, normalized magnitude, and modeled structure together while keeping their horizons separate.

## 7. 07 · Ready

**Check the publish boundary**

Confirm lineage, chart labels, scope line, caveats, and challenge path before calling the recap ready.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Hand it to the audit**

Next: S4E11 · audit a Market Recap for freshness, lineage, missingness, and a real challenge path.
