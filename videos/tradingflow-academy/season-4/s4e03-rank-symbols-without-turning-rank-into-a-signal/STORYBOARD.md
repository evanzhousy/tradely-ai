# S4E03 storyboard

1. **01 · Attention** — Rank shows where to look — Rank Symbols compresses a declared universe into a short inspection queue for the selected session. — visual: `s4e03-attention`
2. **02 · Relative** — Relative is not absolute — A first-place row can move because peers changed; preserve the denominator and the comparison set. — visual: `s4e03-relative`
3. **03 · Fields** — Read the fields behind the row — Open the scope, freshness, flow lens, and coverage before treating a rank difference as useful evidence. — visual: `s4e03-fields`
4. **04 · Candidate** — Promote a candidate to inspection — A candidate earns a drawer review only after the universe and session rules remain intact. — visual: `s4e03-candidate`
5. **05 · Counter** — Ask what could invert the rank — Missing rows, stale context, denominator drift, or a different lens can change relative attention. — visual: `s4e03-counter`
6. **06 · No signal** — Keep rank descriptive — Rank does not assert direction, dealer intent, probability, or a trade decision. — visual: `s4e03-no-signal`
7. **07 · Log** — Record the inspection handoff — Save the rank view, selected row, scope, timestamp, and next check so another analyst can replay it. — visual: `s4e03-log`
8. **08 · Practice** — Open the symbol drawer — Next: S4E04 · open one symbol drawer and check freshness before reading the fields inside. — visual: `s4e03-outro`
