# S4E03 narration outline

Rank Symbols Without Turning Rank into a Signal

## 1. 01 · Attention

**Rank shows where to look**

Rank Symbols compresses a declared universe into a short inspection queue for the selected session.

## 2. 02 · Relative

**Relative is not absolute**

A first-place row can move because peers changed; preserve the denominator and the comparison set.

## 3. 03 · Fields

**Read the fields behind the row**

Open the scope, freshness, flow lens, and coverage before treating a rank difference as useful evidence.

## 4. 04 · Candidate

**Promote a candidate to inspection**

A candidate earns a drawer review only after the universe and session rules remain intact.

## 5. 05 · Counter

**Ask what could invert the rank**

Missing rows, stale context, denominator drift, or a different lens can change relative attention.

## 6. 06 · No signal

**Keep rank descriptive**

Rank does not assert direction, dealer intent, probability, or a trade decision.

## 7. 07 · Log

**Record the inspection handoff**

Save the rank view, selected row, scope, timestamp, and next check so another analyst can replay it.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Open the symbol drawer**

Next: S4E04 · open one symbol drawer and check freshness before reading the fields inside.
