# S1E07 narration outline

DEX and DEI: Compare Directional Impact

## 1. 01 · Two lenses

**Do not collapse direction and magnitude**

DEX is signed tape context. DEI normalizes directional impact for comparison.

## 2. 02 · DEX

**DEX keeps the sign**

Read call-versus-put and buy-versus-sell context without calling it dealer inventory.

## 3. 03 · DEI

**DEI makes scale comparable**

Use magnitude to compare symbols or contracts; preserve the source and horizon.

## 4. 04 · Rank symbols

**A rank row is a candidate**

Relative attention narrows the universe. It does not finish the analysis.

## 5. 05 · Rank contracts

**Move from symbol to contract**

Expiry, strike, moneyness, repetition, and Vol/OI restore specificity.

## 6. 06 · Tape

**Return to the prints**

A direction label still needs quote context, size, timing, and neighboring prints.

## 7. 07 · Compare

**Compare like with like**

Same session · same lens · same horizon · same freshness line.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Direction is evidence, not a decision**

Next: run the full intraday index workflow from discovery to decision.
