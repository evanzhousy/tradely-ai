# S1E19 narration outline

Run a Disagreement Retrospective

## 1. 01 · Freeze

**Lock the original reads**

Save Analyst A and B exactly as written; a retrospective explains the gap without rewriting history.

## 2. 02 · Timeline

**Rebuild the evidence timeline**

Place the print, quote context, neighboring leg, OI snapshot, and follow-through in their observed order.

## 3. 03 · Divergence

**Find the first branch**

Mark the earliest field or step where the reads stopped using the same evidence boundary.

## 4. 04 · Cause

**Classify the cause of the gap**

Data availability, method rule, and interpretation are different fixes; do not treat them as one bug.

## 5. 05 · Counterfactual

**Test the smallest repair**

Change one field or rule at a time and see whether both reads become more comparable.

## 6. 06 · Decision

**Choose the method update**

Keep the original cohort outcome, then version the smallest change that improves reproducibility.

## 7. 07 · Trail

**Preserve the audit trail**

Record original reads, divergence, repair, and remaining uncertainty so the next review can start here.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Make retrospectives compound**

Next: S1E20 · build a review cadence that compounds instead of rewriting old reads.
