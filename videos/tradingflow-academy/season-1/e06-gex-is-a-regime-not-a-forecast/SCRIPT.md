# S1E06 narration outline

GEX Is a Regime, Not a Forecast

## 1. 01 · Start with scope

**GEX is a model view**

Selected date, expiry scope, and prior-close context come first.

## 2. 02 · Positive gamma

**Positive is a regime label**

Gamma exposure describes modeled sensitivity around the map—not a guaranteed outcome.

## 3. 03 · Negative gamma

**Negative is not a signal**

The sign changes the context you inspect; it does not choose a direction for you.

## 4. 04 · Transition

**Find the transition zone**

A zero-crossing is a location in the model, not a magic price target.

## 5. 05 · Walls

**Call Wall and Put Wall frame levels**

Keep the wall label, the expiry, and the selected date beside the chart.

## 6. 06 · Level map

**Read the map as context**

Pair structure with current flow and price evidence before deciding what matters.

## 7. 07 · Invalidation

**Name what would change your read**

A regime can persist while the active evidence changes. Record the disconfirming observation.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Use GEX to ask better questions**

Next: compare DEX direction with DEI magnitude—two lenses, not dealer inventory.
