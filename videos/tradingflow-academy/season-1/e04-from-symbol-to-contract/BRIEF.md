# TradingFlow Academy S1E04

## From Symbol to Contract

### Objective

Teach a new options-flow trader how to move from a ranked underlying symbol to one exact option contract without turning a single metric into a trade signal. TradingFlow is the primary teaching surface.

### Audience

Options traders who can read calls, puts, strikes, and expiries, but still jump from “this symbol is active” to “this is the contract to trade.”

### Promise

By the end, the viewer can narrow a candidate using five evidence dimensions:

1. expiry and days to expiration;
2. strike and current moneyness;
3. repetition across prints;
4. daily volume relative to open interest;
5. exact-contract inspection across Flow, Positioning, and Tradeability.

### Domain guardrails

- Contract Rank rows aggregate the full selected session.
- `Vol/OI = daily volume ÷ open interest` when open interest is positive.
- Vol/OI describes turnover relative to contracts outstanding. It does not prove that positions were opened.
- Moneyness is the latest available print or chain-snapshot label. Full-session premium and flow totals cover the whole selected session.
- Ask and bid shares describe execution-side size share. They are not literal opening or closing labels.
- Expiry, strike, moneyness, repetition, and Vol/OI are evidence dimensions, not a “best contract” score.
- Flow and positioning can use different measurement horizons. The video must identify the mismatch instead of blending them into one conclusion.
- No prediction, “price magnet,” dealer-intent claim, forced-hedging claim, or trade recommendation.

### Format

- 1920 × 1080, 30 fps
- English narration
- Target runtime: 7–8 minutes
- TradingFlow Academy visual continuity with a product-native dark workbench
- Marcus Professional voice for series continuity, subject to external TTS authorization
- Full-color TradingFlow mark on every frame
- Black subtitles on a dedicated light caption rail, never over product data
- Deliver two final renders: with BGM and without BGM

### Evidence policy

Use only local test/demo product captures, frozen examples, or anonymized primitives. Never expose customer data, credentials, or production-only account state.

### Autonomous creative decision

The short-promo energy arc is adapted into chapter-level micro-arcs. A 6–7 minute lesson needs periodic holds and comprehension beats, so the film does not follow a continuous 30–60 second promotional climb.
