# S1E10 storyboard

1. **01 · The hook** — The loudest print can still be wrong — A hedge, spread, roll, or missing follow-through can mimic conviction. — visual: `print-card`
2. **02 · Freeze the observation** — Write down exactly what traded — Keep symbol, contract, side, size, session, and source visible before interpreting it. — visual: `tape`
3. **03 · Check the neighboring leg** — A paired trade changes the question — Call and put legs together can describe a spread or roll—not one directional bet. — visual: `spread`
4. **04 · Separate flow from structure** — OI is not a live opening ledger — Use the later snapshot as evidence about persistence, not as a time machine. — visual: `ledger`
5. **05 · Re-read the chain** — Moneyness and expiry restore context — A contract is not comparable until strike, DTE, and neighboring strikes are in scope. — visual: `chain`
6. **06 · Ask for follow-through** — The next observation has to earn the claim — Compare same-session prints and the next cleared snapshot without backfilling certainty. — visual: `followthrough`
7. **07 · Downgrade cleanly** — Write the contradiction — If the alternative explanation remains plausible, keep the observation and lower the claim. — visual: `invalid`
8. **08 · Practice** — Turn false positives into a process — Season 1 continues with a repeatable scanner: same universe, same filters, new session. — visual: `outro-autopsy`
