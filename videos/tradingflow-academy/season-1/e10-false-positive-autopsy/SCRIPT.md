# S1E10 narration outline

False-Positive Autopsy

## 1. 01 · The hook

**The loudest print can still be wrong**

A hedge, spread, roll, or missing follow-through can mimic conviction.

## 2. 02 · Freeze the observation

**Write down exactly what traded**

Keep symbol, contract, side, size, session, and source visible before interpreting it.

## 3. 03 · Check the neighboring leg

**A paired trade changes the question**

Call and put legs together can describe a spread or roll—not one directional bet.

## 4. 04 · Separate flow from structure

**OI is not a live opening ledger**

Use the later snapshot as evidence about persistence, not as a time machine.

## 5. 05 · Re-read the chain

**Moneyness and expiry restore context**

A contract is not comparable until strike, DTE, and neighboring strikes are in scope.

## 6. 06 · Ask for follow-through

**The next observation has to earn the claim**

Compare same-session prints and the next cleared snapshot without backfilling certainty.

## 7. 07 · Downgrade cleanly

**Write the contradiction**

If the alternative explanation remains plausible, keep the observation and lower the claim.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Turn false positives into a process**

Season 1 continues with a repeatable scanner: same universe, same filters, new session.
