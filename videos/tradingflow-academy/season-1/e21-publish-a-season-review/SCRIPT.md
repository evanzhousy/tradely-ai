# S1E21 narration outline

Publish a Season Review

## 1. 01 · Purpose

**Review the method, not the hit rate**

Summarize the evidence workflow, not a leaderboard of directional outcomes.

## 2. 02 · Coverage

**Show what the season actually covered**

Symbols, contracts, sessions, cohorts, reports, blind replays, and retrospectives define the sample boundary.

## 3. 03 · Learning

**Name the method improvements**

Fixed recipes, explicit scope, challengeable reports, and versioned repairs are the durable outputs.

## 4. 04 · Gaps

**Keep unresolved items visible**

Missing fields, limited samples, stale context, and unresolved reads belong in the published review.

## 5. 05 · Evidence

**Separate evidence from inference**

A season can show better reproducibility without claiming a forecast advantage.

## 6. 06 · Boundaries

**State what the review cannot say**

No intent, direction, probability, or universal performance claim follows from this illustrative season.

## 7. 07 · Next

**Give the next season a testable starting point**

Carry the review packet and open a new hypothesis instead of rewriting Season 1.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Close the season with a clean handoff**
