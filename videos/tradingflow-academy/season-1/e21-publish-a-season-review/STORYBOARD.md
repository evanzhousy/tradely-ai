# S1E21 storyboard

1. **01 · Purpose** — Review the method, not the hit rate — Summarize the evidence workflow, not a leaderboard of directional outcomes. — visual: `season-purpose`
2. **02 · Coverage** — Show what the season actually covered — Symbols, contracts, sessions, cohorts, reports, blind replays, and retrospectives define the sample boundary. — visual: `season-coverage`
3. **03 · Learning** — Name the method improvements — Fixed recipes, explicit scope, challengeable reports, and versioned repairs are the durable outputs. — visual: `season-learning`
4. **04 · Gaps** — Keep unresolved items visible — Missing fields, limited samples, stale context, and unresolved reads belong in the published review. — visual: `season-gaps`
5. **05 · Evidence** — Separate evidence from inference — A season can show better reproducibility without claiming a forecast advantage. — visual: `season-evidence`
6. **06 · Boundaries** — State what the review cannot say — No intent, direction, probability, or universal performance claim follows from this illustrative season. — visual: `season-boundary`
7. **07 · Next** — Give the next season a testable starting point — Carry the review packet and open a new hypothesis instead of rewriting Season 1. — visual: `season-next`
8. **08 · Practice** — Close the season with a clean handoff — Next: S2E01 · start a new season with a new hypothesis. — visual: `outro-season-review`
