# S1E09 narration outline

The Single-Stock Event Workflow

## 1. 01 · Event clock

**Start with the event**

Earnings date, session, and timezone define the window you are researching.

## 2. 02 · DTE

**DTE changes the comparison**

A two-day contract and a sixty-day contract are not interchangeable observations.

## 3. 03 · IV

**Put volatility in context**

Compare term structure and moneyness before treating an IV print as unusual.

## 4. 04 · Moneyness

**Map the strike to the underlying**

Distance from spot is a coordinate, not a directional conclusion.

## 5. 05 · Structure

**Inspect the standing map**

Use OI, delta-OI, GEX, and walls as separate context layers.

## 6. 06 · Current flow

**Then return to today's tape**

Use Option Trades Historical to preserve the session and sequence.

## 7. 07 · Evidence ledger

**Record what would change your mind**

Event research stays useful when the observation, scope, and invalidation are explicit.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Build the event note**

Season 1 closes the loop: discover, inspect, validate, compare, invalidate, replay, decide.
