# S1E20 storyboard

1. **01 · Cadence** — Review before memory rewrites the story — A repeatable cadence keeps original reads, evidence gaps, and repairs visible across the season. — visual: `cadence-card`
2. **02 · Triggers** — Define when a review starts — New cohort, analyst disagreement, missing field, method change, or an invalidation check can open a review. — visual: `trigger-card`
3. **03 · Packet** — Use the same review packet — Original reads, scope, timeline, first divergence, repair, version, and remaining uncertainty travel together. — visual: `packet-card`
4. **04 · Levels** — Match the review to the problem — Quick field check, full retrospective, and rubric review are different levels—not one giant ceremony. — visual: `levels-card`
5. **05 · Ownership** — Give the repair a clear owner — Record who checks the field, who updates the method, and who verifies the next replay. — visual: `owner-card`
6. **06 · Measure** — Measure process health, not prediction wins — Track missingness, reproducibility, unresolved states, and repair latency—not directional hit rate. — visual: `measure-card`
7. **07 · Compound** — Carry repairs forward without rewriting history — New versions improve future reports while old cohorts remain interpretable under their original rules. — visual: `compound-card`
8. **08 · Practice** — Make the cadence part of TradingFlow — Next: S1E21 · publish a season review without turning it into a scorecard. — visual: `outro-cadence`
