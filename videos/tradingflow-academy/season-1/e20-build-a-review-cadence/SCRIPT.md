# S1E20 narration outline

Build a Review Cadence That Compounds

## 1. 01 · Cadence

**Review before memory rewrites the story**

A repeatable cadence keeps original reads, evidence gaps, and repairs visible across the season.

## 2. 02 · Triggers

**Define when a review starts**

New cohort, analyst disagreement, missing field, method change, or an invalidation check can open a review.

## 3. 03 · Packet

**Use the same review packet**

Original reads, scope, timeline, first divergence, repair, version, and remaining uncertainty travel together.

## 4. 04 · Levels

**Match the review to the problem**

Quick field check, full retrospective, and rubric review are different levels—not one giant ceremony.

## 5. 05 · Ownership

**Give the repair a clear owner**

Record who checks the field, who updates the method, and who verifies the next replay.

## 6. 06 · Measure

**Measure process health, not prediction wins**

Track missingness, reproducibility, unresolved states, and repair latency—not directional hit rate.

## 7. 07 · Compound

**Carry repairs forward without rewriting history**

New versions improve future reports while old cohorts remain interpretable under their original rules.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Make the cadence part of TradingFlow**
