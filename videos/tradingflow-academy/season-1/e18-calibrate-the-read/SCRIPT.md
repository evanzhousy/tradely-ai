# S1E18 narration outline

Calibrate the Read Without Collapsing Nuance

## 1. 01 · Goal

**Calibrate the method, not the answer**

Use the same evidence boundaries and resolution vocabulary while preserving legitimate uncertainty.

## 2. 02 · Anchors

**Agree on the shared facts**

Timestamp, contract, side, size, session, source, and freshness are anchors before interpretation begins.

## 3. 03 · Vocabulary

**Define the resolution words**

Retained means evidence still fits; lowered means an alternative survived; unresolved means the check is incomplete.

## 4. 04 · Layers

**Keep facts and inferences separate**

A shared observation can support different provisional readings when the missing check is named.

## 5. 05 · Compare

**Calibrate the boundary of disagreement**

If both analysts use the same facts but different resolution, log the exact step that separates them.

## 6. 06 · Uncertainty

**Let unresolved stay unresolved**

Do not convert an incomplete check into a forced yes or no just to make the dashboard tidy.

## 7. 07 · Version

**Version the rubric when it changes**

A new definition or check belongs in a new rubric version so old reports remain interpretable.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Make calibration a shared habit**
