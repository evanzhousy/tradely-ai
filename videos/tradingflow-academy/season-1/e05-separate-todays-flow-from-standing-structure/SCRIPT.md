# S1E05 narration outline

Separate Today's Flow from Standing Structure

## 1. 01 · The split

**Today's tape is not the whole position**

Flow is session evidence. Structure is the backdrop.

## 2. 02 · Volume

**Volume answers: what traded today?**

A print is an execution observation, bounded by its session.

## 3. 03 · Open interest

**Open interest answers a different question**

OI is reported after the session; it is not a live position ledger.

## 4. 04 · Delta-OI

**Use delta-OI as a change, not a verdict**

Compare snapshots, then keep the sign and the scope visible.

## 5. 05 · Vol/OI

**Turnover is not intent**

High Vol/OI means activity is large relative to standing OI—not that new positions were opened.

## 6. 06 · Walls

**Walls are structure context**

Call Wall and Put Wall can frame a map; they do not forecast the next print.

## 7. 07 · Freshness

**Write the freshness line**

Session · as-of timestamp · expiry scope · source lens. Make the evidence auditable.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Keep the two ledgers separate**

Next: GEX as a regime, with selected-date and expiry context intact.

## Practice

GEX is a regime, not a forecast.
