# S1E05 storyboard

1. **01 · The split** — Today's tape is not the whole position — Flow is session evidence. Structure is the backdrop. — visual: `split`
2. **02 · Volume** — Volume answers: what traded today? — A print is an execution observation, bounded by its session. — visual: `bars`
3. **03 · Open interest** — Open interest answers a different question — OI is reported after the session; it is not a live position ledger. — visual: `ledger`
4. **04 · Delta-OI** — Use delta-OI as a change, not a verdict — Compare snapshots, then keep the sign and the scope visible. — visual: `delta`
5. **05 · Vol/OI** — Turnover is not intent — High Vol/OI means activity is large relative to standing OI—not that new positions were opened. — visual: `ratio`
6. **06 · Walls** — Walls are structure context — Call Wall and Put Wall can frame a map; they do not forecast the next print. — visual: `walls`
7. **07 · Freshness** — Write the freshness line — Session · as-of timestamp · expiry scope · source lens. Make the evidence auditable. — visual: `clock`
8. **08 · Practice** — Keep the two ledgers separate — Next: GEX as a regime, with selected-date and expiry context intact. — visual: `outro`
