## Intent

Create the first eight-minute TradingFlow Academy episode, **Why Options Flow
Traders Get Faked Out**. Lead with the viewer's interpretation problem before
introducing the platform. Fairly explain the strengths and limits of
tape-first, structure-first, and alert-first approaches, then demonstrate the
TradingFlow workflow:

> Discover → Inspect → Validate → Compare → Decide

The App is the proof, not a decorative product cutaway. The episode should feel
like a calm analyst correcting a dangerous shortcut, not an aggressive product
commercial.

## Notes

- White background, solid black text, and no gradient text.
- Chart gradients may be used only within one coherent color family per chart.
- Use actual current module names and routes.
- Do not claim competitors lack Flow, GEX, contract analytics, or multi-tool workflows.
- Do not show named competitors in the default cut; compare method categories unless a later review explicitly requests a named comparison.
- Do not teach a numeric DEX formula until the multiplier contract is aligned.
- Net DEX is session-flow direction, not dealer inventory.
- Net DEI and ΔOI DEI are magnitudes; direction is read separately.
- GEX is selected-date prior-close modeled structure, not an intraday forecast.
- The final Studio preview remains user-gated before rendering.
