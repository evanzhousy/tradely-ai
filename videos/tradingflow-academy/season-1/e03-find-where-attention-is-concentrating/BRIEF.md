## Intent

TradingFlow Academy Season 1, Episode 3 teaches the discovery step that comes
before inspecting an individual option print. The lesson begins with the
common mistake of chasing the largest premium number, then shows how
TradingFlow Rank · Symbols compresses a complete canonical symbol universe
into a relative, session-scoped list of candidates.

The platform remains the primary teaching tool. The viewer should leave able
to select a session, choose a ranking question, compare magnitude and
direction separately, verify provenance, and open a symbol for deeper
inspection without treating rank order as a forecast.

## Notes

- Rank · Symbols is built from its own complete canonical full-chain symbol universe. It must never be described as grouped Contract Rank rows.
- Rank is the discovery layer; Option Trades is the tape-validation layer.
- “Latest” means the most recent completed or active session. Before the open, on weekends, and on holidays, that may be the prior completed session.
- The Symbols view mixes a full-chain structural snapshot with a separately timestamped lightweight flow overlay. Structure and flow provenance must remain distinct.
- Net DEI is magnitude. Direction must be read separately from Net DEX or Sentiment.
- Net DEX describes signed session trade-tape direction, not dealer inventory, a price level, or forced hedging.
- A ranked symbol is a candidate for inspection, not a signal, forecast, or recommendation.
