# TradingFlow Luma Research Shell

## Concept angle

The frame behaves like a discovery workbench. A broad symbol universe
compresses into a ranked cohort, then the composition separates relative
magnitude, session direction, and structural provenance before one candidate
is opened for deeper inspection.

## Frame composition

- Keep the approved white canvas and solid black body text.
- Use app-like toolbar chrome at the top and a dedicated caption surface at the bottom.
- Scale authentic TradingFlow primitives for video: large headings, soft elevated cards, compact analytical rows, and pill chips.
- Keep data-dense zones compact while leaving one clear explanatory zone per scene.
- Anchor the product surface to an edge; use a second focal point for the active sorter, session scope, provenance label, or candidate state.

## Typography

- Inter carries headings and explanation.
- Headings use 64–92px at weight 700.
- Body copy uses 28–38px at weight 400.
- JetBrains Mono carries date, time, strike, expiry, price, premium, and evidence states.
- Text remains solid. Never use gradient text.

## Product and data treatment

- Preserve the authentic TradingFlow screenshots and frame them as app windows.
- Reconstructed rows may use only values visible in the authentic screenshots, or clearly labeled illustrative values when explaining mechanics rather than a market claim.
- Green and red communicate signed session direction; blue communicates product focus, selection, and sequence.
- Gradients are allowed only inside data bars or progress marks, using one coherent blue family.
- `UNIVERSE`, `SESSION`, `MAGNITUDE`, `DIRECTION`, `PROVENANCE`, and `CANDIDATE` must read as explicit analytical states.

## Motion

- Use critically damped entrances for app chrome and rows.
- Move each screenshot as one stable surface; focus changes happen through callouts, cursor motion, and coordinate-targeted zoom.
- The universe-to-candidate compression uses a narrowing funnel that never turns into a buy or sell arrow.
- Magnitude and direction remain visually separate throughout the lesson.

## Do

- Show the selected session and latest/completed-session meaning wherever rank order appears.
- Pair every magnitude metric with a separate direction read.
- Keep subtitles isolated from product UI and charts.
- Use the full-color TradingFlow logo in every frame.

## Don't

- Do not describe Symbol Rank as grouped Contract Rank rows.
- Do not describe Net DEX as dealer inventory, support/resistance, or forced hedging.
- Do not give Net DEI a directional sign; direction lives in Net DEX and Sentiment.
- Do not equate rank order, premium, or one busy session with future price direction.
- Do not use serif type, neon finance HUDs, gradient text, or decorative chart junk.
