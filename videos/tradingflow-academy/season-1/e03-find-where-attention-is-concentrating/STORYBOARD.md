## Hallmark design system

- macrostructure: Workbench
- genre: modern-minimal
- tone: technical + austere
- theme: TradingFlow Cobalt
- surface: cool paper, ink-first hierarchy, one primary blue accent
- cards: 14px radius, hairline borders, restrained single-layer shadows
- product evidence: authentic TradingFlow captures, followed by video-scale analytical primitives
- motion: information-carrying transforms and opacity only; no bounce, elastic overshoot, or decorative drift
- typography: TradingFlow’s local Inter remains the brand face; JetBrains Mono is limited to sessions, metrics, ranks, and provenance
- subtitles: black ink on a separate cool-neutral rail

## Video direction

The viewer should feel a noisy scanner becoming an orderly research process.
The visual rhythm is controlled–compress–inspect–hold: early scenes contrast
raw scale with relative impact, the middle scenes narrow a full universe into
a shortlist, and the final scenes slow down around provenance and the next
research question. Authentic app captures act as evidence; reconstructed
tables, sorters, and data marks explain the mechanism at video scale.
