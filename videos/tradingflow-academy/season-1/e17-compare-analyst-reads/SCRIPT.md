# S1E17 narration outline

Compare Analyst Reads

## 1. 01 · Pair

**Start with two independent reads**

Give Analyst A and Analyst B the same SPX 5900 C packet and keep each note sealed until both are written.

## 2. 02 · Scope

**Make the shared packet explicit**

Same contract, session, lens, horizon, quote context, and freshness rule form the comparison boundary.

## 3. 03 · Reads

**Put the resolutions side by side**

A retained claim and a lowered claim can both be faithful to the same observed print.

## 4. 04 · Divergence

**Find the first field that differs**

Trace the disagreement to selection, missingness, scope, or interpretation before debating the conclusion.

## 5. 05 · Layers

**Separate facts from interpretations**

Timestamp, contract, side, and size are shared facts; persistence and resolution are interpretive layers.

## 6. 06 · Evidence

**Add the check that can narrow the gap**

Use the same neighboring-leg, OI, follow-through, and invalidation checks on both reads.

## 7. 07 · Log

**Record agreement without erasing nuance**

Log what matched, what stayed unresolved, and which rule or field needs clarification.

**Make disagreement useful**
