# S1E15 narration outline

Write a Replay Report

## 1. 01 · Question

**Open with the question, not the answer**

What happened to SPX 5900 C across the replay cohort, and what would change the claim?

## 2. 02 · Scope

**Put the recipe beside the result**

Universe, expiry, moneyness, side, Vol/OI, lens, and as-of rule define what the report can say.

## 3. 03 · Observation

**Separate the print from the interpretation**

Report the timestamp, contract, side, and size first; label the claim as a resolution, not intent.

## 4. 04 · Cohort

**Show every row that made the sample**

Sessions A, B, and C remain visible so a reader can inspect selection and missingness.

## 5. 05 · Contradictions

**Put the disconfirming evidence in the report**

Neighboring legs, changed quotes, stale timestamps, and missing follow-through belong beside the claim.

## 6. 06 · Resolution

**State what the evidence supports**

Retained, lowered, or unresolved is a defensible resolution; direction and intent stay unasserted.

## 7. 07 · Reproduce

**Make the next analyst able to rerun it**

Record the recipe version, selected sessions, source lens, and the exact fields used in the comparison.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Publish the challenge surface**
