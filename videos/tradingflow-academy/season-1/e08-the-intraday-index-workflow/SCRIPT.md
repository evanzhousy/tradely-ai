# S1E08 narration outline

The Intraday Index Workflow

## 1. 01 · Discover

**Start broad, then narrow**

Rank Symbols shows where attention is concentrating in the selected session.

## 2. 02 · Inspect

**Open the symbol drawer**

See the scope, the lens, and the current evidence before forming a story.

## 3. 03 · Validate

**Check the contract details**

Move through expiry, strike, moneyness, repetition, and quote context.

## 4. 04 · Compare

**Compare the neighboring evidence**

One print is weak; a coherent sequence is stronger, still uncertain.

## 5. 05 · Decide

**Decide what to record**

A research note can be useful without becoming a trade instruction.

## 6. 06 · Invalidate

**Write the disconfirming case**

What observation would make your current interpretation less useful?

## 7. 07 · Replay

**Re-run the same loop**

Reproducibility is part of the method: same filters, same scope, new timestamp.
