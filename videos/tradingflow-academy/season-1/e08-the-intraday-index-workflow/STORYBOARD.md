# S1E08 storyboard

1. **01 · Discover** — Start broad, then narrow — Rank Symbols shows where attention is concentrating in the selected session. — visual: `discover`
2. **02 · Inspect** — Open the symbol drawer — See the scope, the lens, and the current evidence before forming a story. — visual: `inspect`
3. **03 · Validate** — Check the contract details — Move through expiry, strike, moneyness, repetition, and quote context. — visual: `validate`
4. **04 · Compare** — Compare the neighboring evidence — One print is weak; a coherent sequence is stronger, still uncertain. — visual: `compare`
5. **05 · Decide** — Decide what to record — A research note can be useful without becoming a trade instruction. — visual: `decide`
6. **06 · Invalidate** — Write the disconfirming case — What observation would make your current interpretation less useful? — visual: `invalid`
7. **07 · Replay** — Re-run the same loop — Reproducibility is part of the method: same filters, same scope, new timestamp. — visual: `replay`
8. **08 · Practice** — The loop is the product — Next: S1E09 · The Single-Stock Event Workflow, with DTE and IV in view. — visual: `outro-loop`
