# S1E14 narration outline

Build a Cohort of Replays

## 1. 01 · Cohort

**One replay is still a clue**

E13 compared two sessions. Add a third while keeping the contract, recipe, and log fields fixed.

## 2. 02 · Recipe

**Freeze one recipe for every row**

Universe SPX · 45 DTE · ATM ±5% · ASK side · Vol/OI ≥ 1× · same lens and as-of rule.

## 3. 03 · Select

**Keep the whole cohort**

Session A, B, and C stay in the set even when size, timing, or follow-through disagrees.

## 4. 04 · Outcomes

**Track the fields that can change the claim**

For every row: neighboring leg, OI persistence, next snapshot, and claim resolution.

## 5. 05 · Compare

**Describe the sample without forecasting**

Two retained and one downgraded is a record of this cohort—not a probability or a price target.

## 6. 06 · Outlier

**Inspect the outlier before excluding it**

A changed quote, stale timestamp, or contract mismatch can explain drift; label it, do not erase it.

## 7. 07 · Version

**Separate method changes from results**

If the recipe changes, open a new cohort; never blend old and new rules into one result.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Make replay cohorts a habit**
