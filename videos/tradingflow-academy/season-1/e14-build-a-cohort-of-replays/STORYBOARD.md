# S1E14 storyboard

1. **01 · Cohort** — One replay is still a clue — E13 compared two sessions. Add a third while keeping the contract, recipe, and log fields fixed. — visual: `cohort-card`
2. **02 · Recipe** — Freeze one recipe for every row — Universe SPX · 45 DTE · ATM ±5% · ASK side · Vol/OI ≥ 1× · same lens and as-of rule. — visual: `scope`
3. **03 · Select** — Keep the whole cohort — Session A, B, and C stay in the set even when size, timing, or follow-through disagrees. — visual: `cohort-table`
4. **04 · Outcomes** — Track the fields that can change the claim — For every row: neighboring leg, OI persistence, next snapshot, and claim resolution. — visual: `outcome-ledger`
5. **05 · Compare** — Describe the sample without forecasting — Two retained and one downgraded is a record of this cohort—not a probability or a price target. — visual: `cohort-compare`
6. **06 · Outlier** — Inspect the outlier before excluding it — A changed quote, stale timestamp, or contract mismatch can explain drift; label it, do not erase it. — visual: `outlier-card`
7. **07 · Version** — Separate method changes from results — If the recipe changes, open a new cohort; never blend old and new rules into one result. — visual: `version-card`
8. **08 · Practice** — Make replay cohorts a habit — Next: S1E15 · write a replay report another analyst can challenge. — visual: `outro-cohort`
