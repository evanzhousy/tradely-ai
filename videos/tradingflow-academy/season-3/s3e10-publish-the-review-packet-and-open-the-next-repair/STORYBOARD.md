# S3E10 storyboard

1. **01 · Question** — Publish the question and boundary — State what the Season 3 packet tested, which rows are in scope, and what the evidence cannot establish. — visual: `s3e10-review`
2. **02 · Packet** — Bundle the evidence trail — Keep identity, scope, freshness, sequence, resolutions, challenges, and recipe version in one inspectable packet. — visual: `packet-card`
3. **03 · Lineage** — Keep every repair traceable — Link the published cohort, blind replay, calibrated vocabulary, and next version without rewriting earlier reads. — visual: `compound-card`
4. **04 · Gaps** — Publish the unresolved inventory — Missing fields, limited rows, stale context, and unresolved states remain visible beside the conclusions. — visual: `s3e10-gaps`
5. **05 · Ownership** — Assign the next repair question — Make the check, update, replay, and review owners visible before the next cohort begins. — visual: `owner-card`
6. **06 · Rerun** — Make another analyst able to rerun it — Record recipe, sessions, fields, vocabulary, and invalidation so the packet can be challenged without ceremony. — visual: `report-reproduce`
7. **07 · Handoff** — Open the next version carefully — A new repair belongs to future rows; the published history stays under its original rules and uncertainty. — visual: `s3e10-handoff`
8. **08 · Practice** — Carry the packet into the next cohort — Next: S3E11 · run the next cohort with the updated packet and keep the repair lineage visible. — visual: `s3e10-outro`
