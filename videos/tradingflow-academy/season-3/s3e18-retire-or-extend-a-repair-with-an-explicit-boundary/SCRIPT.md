# S3E18 narration outline

Retire or Extend a Repair with an Explicit Boundary

## 1. 01 · Question

**Define the retirement test**

Ask whether the repair improved inspectability, rerunability, and challengeability within its declared scope.

## 2. 02 · Evidence

**Inspect the comparison packet**

Review cohort identity, aligned inputs, unresolved states, missingness, lineage, and owner before choosing a path.

## 3. 03 · Extend

**Name the extension case**

Extend only when the question remains open, the packet is rerunnable, and the next rows can preserve the same boundary.

## 4. 04 · Retire

**Name the stop case**

Retire when scope drift, stale context, or missing required fields prevent a fair next test.

## 5. 05 · Boundary

**Write the next scope**

Record future rows, recipe version, required fields, invalidations, and what stays historical before the next run.

## 6. 06 · Ownership

**Assign the decision owner**

Make the reviewer, packet maintainer, replay operator, and escalation path visible for the next version.

## 7. 07 · Record

**Publish the decision**

Keep extend, retire, unresolved, and missing as process states; do not convert them into performance claims.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Open the next method change**

Next: S3E19 · publish the method change without rewriting the cohort history or blending boundaries.
