# S3E18 storyboard

1. **01 · Question** — Define the retirement test — Ask whether the repair improved inspectability, rerunability, and challengeability within its declared scope. — visual: `s3e18-question`
2. **02 · Evidence** — Inspect the comparison packet — Review cohort identity, aligned inputs, unresolved states, missingness, lineage, and owner before choosing a path. — visual: `s3e18-evidence`
3. **03 · Extend** — Name the extension case — Extend only when the question remains open, the packet is rerunnable, and the next rows can preserve the same boundary. — visual: `s3e18-extend`
4. **04 · Retire** — Name the stop case — Retire when scope drift, stale context, or missing required fields prevent a fair next test. — visual: `s3e18-retire`
5. **05 · Boundary** — Write the next scope — Record future rows, recipe version, required fields, invalidations, and what stays historical before the next run. — visual: `s3e18-boundary`
6. **06 · Ownership** — Assign the decision owner — Make the reviewer, packet maintainer, replay operator, and escalation path visible for the next version. — visual: `s3e18-owner`
7. **07 · Record** — Publish the decision — Keep extend, retire, unresolved, and missing as process states; do not convert them into performance claims. — visual: `s3e18-record`
8. **08 · Practice** — Open the next method change — Next: S3E19 · publish the method change without rewriting the cohort history or blending boundaries. — visual: `s3e18-outro`
