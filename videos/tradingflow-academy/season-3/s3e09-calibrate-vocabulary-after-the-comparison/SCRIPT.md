# S3E09 narration outline

Calibrate Vocabulary After the Comparison

## 1. 01 · Terms

**Start with the words that changed**

Collect the labels both records used and separate shared evidence from analyst shorthand.

## 2. 02 · States

**Define the allowed resolution states**

Retained, lowered, unresolved, and missing describe process outcomes without importing direction or intent.

## 3. 03 · Boundary

**Align the evidence boundary**

Compare the fields each state requires: scope, freshness, quote context, contract identity, and invalidation.

## 4. 04 · Language

**Keep words tied to fields**

A stronger word needs stronger evidence; do not let vocabulary outrun the packet that supports it.

## 5. 05 · Uncertainty

**Preserve what remains unresolved**

Calibration narrows ambiguity but does not turn a missing field into a probability or a forecast.

## 6. 06 · Test

**Challenge the revised rubric**

Run one example through the calibrated terms and record what would still lower the claim.

## 7. 07 · Publish

**Version the shared vocabulary**

Record the rubric, examples, unresolved edges, and next repair question so another analyst can rerun it.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Open the next review packet**

Next: S3E10 · publish the review packet and open the next repair without rewriting the comparison history.
