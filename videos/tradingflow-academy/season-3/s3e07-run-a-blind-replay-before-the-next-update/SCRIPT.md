# S3E07 narration outline

Run a Blind Replay Before the Next Update

## 1. 01 · Blind start

**Hide the prior resolution**

Open the same question with the published cohort outcome hidden from the fresh read.

## 2. 02 · Scope

**Keep the recipe and scope fixed**

Carry symbol, contract, session, source lens, and repaired version into the replay without changing the test.

## 3. 03 · Observation

**Write the fresh observation first**

Record timestamp, contract, side, size, quote context, and missingness before opening the old report.

## 4. 04 · Resolve

**Lock the independent note**

Use retained, lowered, or unresolved as explicit process states; do not smuggle direction into the resolution.

## 5. 05 · Reveal

**Compare only after the note is locked**

Reveal the published cohort after the fresh read so agreement and disagreement remain inspectable.

## 6. 06 · Diagnose

**Treat disagreement as a diagnostic**

A different resolution points to a field, scope, or method step to clarify—not an analyst who won.

## 7. 07 · Audit

**Trace the step that changed**

Compare selection, missingness, recipe version, invalidation, and lineage before changing the next rule.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Carry the replay into the next comparison**

Next: S3E08 · compare the blind replay with the published cohort and keep both resolutions visible.
