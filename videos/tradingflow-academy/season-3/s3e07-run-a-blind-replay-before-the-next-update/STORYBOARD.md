# S3E07 storyboard

1. **01 · Blind start** — Hide the prior resolution — Open the same question with the published cohort outcome hidden from the fresh read. — visual: `blind-cover`
2. **02 · Scope** — Keep the recipe and scope fixed — Carry symbol, contract, session, source lens, and repaired version into the replay without changing the test. — visual: `scope`
3. **03 · Observation** — Write the fresh observation first — Record timestamp, contract, side, size, quote context, and missingness before opening the old report. — visual: `blind-observation`
4. **04 · Resolve** — Lock the independent note — Use retained, lowered, or unresolved as explicit process states; do not smuggle direction into the resolution. — visual: `uncertainty-card`
5. **05 · Reveal** — Compare only after the note is locked — Reveal the published cohort after the fresh read so agreement and disagreement remain inspectable. — visual: `blind-reveal`
6. **06 · Diagnose** — Treat disagreement as a diagnostic — A different resolution points to a field, scope, or method step to clarify—not an analyst who won. — visual: `blind-disagreement`
7. **07 · Audit** — Trace the step that changed — Compare selection, missingness, recipe version, invalidation, and lineage before changing the next rule. — visual: `blind-audit`
8. **08 · Practice** — Carry the replay into the next comparison — Next: S3E08 · compare the blind replay with the published cohort and keep both resolutions visible. — visual: `s3e07-outro`
