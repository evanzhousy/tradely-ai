# S3E15 narration outline

Decide Whether the Repair Earns Another Cohort

## 1. 01 · Question

**Set the decision rule**

Ask whether the repair made the process more inspectable and rerunnable, not whether it produced a preferred direction.

## 2. 02 · Evidence

**Check the packet is complete**

Verify identity, scope, freshness, sequence, resolutions, challenges, lineage, and missingness before opening another cohort.

## 3. 03 · Movement

**Review process movement**

Compare reproducibility, missingness, unresolved states, and repair latency without turning them into a scorecard.

## 4. 04 · Gaps

**Keep the unresolved boundary**

A fair decision leaves limited rows, stale context, and unresolved checks visible beside the recommendation.

## 5. 05 · Choice

**Keep, narrow, or version**

Retain the repair, narrow one field, or version the next change; never blend rule versions into one cohort.

## 6. 06 · Ownership

**Assign the next cohort owner**

Make the reviewer, packet maintainer, replay operator, and escalation path visible before the next run.

## 7. 07 · Boundary

**Freeze the next test**

Set future-row scope, recipe version, required fields, and invalidation before any new row can influence the method.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Open the next cohort**

Next: S3E16 · run the next cohort under the retained repair boundary and keep this decision historical.
