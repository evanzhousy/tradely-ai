# S3E08 storyboard

1. **01 · Pair** — Open both records side by side — Bring the locked blind note and the published cohort row together only after the fresh read is sealed. — visual: `compare-card`
2. **02 · Scope** — Align the comparison boundary — Confirm symbol, contract, session, source lens, recipe version, and as-of line before comparing resolutions. — visual: `scope`
3. **03 · Shared** — Compare shared facts first — Mark what both records observe in common before asking why their process states differ. — visual: `s3e03-shared`
4. **04 · Resolution** — Name the changed state — Retained, lowered, unresolved, and missing are explicit states—not a hidden performance score. — visual: `report-resolution`
5. **05 · Divergence** — Locate the first changed field — Trace the earliest difference in selection, freshness, quote context, contract identity, or invalidation. — visual: `blind-disagreement`
6. **06 · Layers** — Separate evidence from interpretation — Keep shared facts and scope stable while the provisional read remains labeled as an analyst-layer note. — visual: `definitions-card`
7. **07 · Log** — Version the comparison result — Record agreement, disagreement, unresolved fields, and any repair question without rewriting the published cohort. — visual: `blind-log`
8. **08 · Practice** — Carry the calibration question forward — Next: S3E09 · calibrate vocabulary after the comparison while preserving uncertainty. — visual: `s3e08-outro`
