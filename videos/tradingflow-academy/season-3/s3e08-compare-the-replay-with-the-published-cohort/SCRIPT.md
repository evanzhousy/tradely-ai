# S3E08 narration outline

Compare the Replay with the Published Cohort

## 1. 01 · Pair

**Open both records side by side**

Bring the locked blind note and the published cohort row together only after the fresh read is sealed.

## 2. 02 · Scope

**Align the comparison boundary**

Confirm symbol, contract, session, source lens, recipe version, and as-of line before comparing resolutions.

## 3. 03 · Shared

**Compare shared facts first**

Mark what both records observe in common before asking why their process states differ.

## 4. 04 · Resolution

**Name the changed state**

Retained, lowered, unresolved, and missing are explicit states—not a hidden performance score.

## 5. 05 · Divergence

**Locate the first changed field**

Trace the earliest difference in selection, freshness, quote context, contract identity, or invalidation.

## 6. 06 · Layers

**Separate evidence from interpretation**

Keep shared facts and scope stable while the provisional read remains labeled as an analyst-layer note.

## 7. 07 · Log

**Version the comparison result**

Record agreement, disagreement, unresolved fields, and any repair question without rewriting the published cohort.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Carry the calibration question forward**

Next: S3E09 · calibrate vocabulary after the comparison while preserving uncertainty.
