# S3E03 narration outline

Compare the Same Packet Across Analysts

## 1. 01 · Handoff

**Open the controlled packet**

Use S3E02's frozen identity, fields, sequence, and challenges as the shared starting point.

## 2. 02 · Assignment

**Give both analysts the same inputs**

No hidden fields, changed expiry, or swapped session should enter one analyst's read.

## 3. 03 · Read A

**Let Analyst A write independently**

Record the observation, scope, missingness, and provisional resolution before seeing another note.

## 4. 04 · Read B

**Let Analyst B write independently**

A second read can preserve the same facts while naming a different unresolved field.

## 5. 05 · Shared

**Compare shared facts first**

Align the contract, source lens, timestamp, and observed sequence before comparing interpretations.

## 6. 06 · Divergence

**Locate the first split**

Mark the earliest field where evidence or method diverged; do not jump to the final conclusion.

## 7. 07 · Resolution

**Keep disagreement inspectable**

Retain both notes, label the unresolved state, and carry one repair question into the next review.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Open the repair loop**

Next: S3E04 · repair the first divergence without rewriting either analyst's historical read.
