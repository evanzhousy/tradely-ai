# S3E06 narration outline

Publish the Cohort Report with Repair Lineage

## 1. 01 · Question

**Open with the question, not the score**

State what the repaired cohort was meant to test and what evidence could still lower the claim.

## 2. 02 · Scope

**Put the repair beside the result**

Show symbol, contract, session range, recipe version, source lens, and as-of rules before the rows.

## 3. 03 · Rows

**Show every row in the cohort**

Keep retained, lowered, unresolved, and missing rows visible so selection cannot disappear into a summary.

## 4. 04 · Lineage

**Trace the repair boundary**

Link each row to the method version that produced it; keep the old cohort historical and unchanged.

## 5. 05 · Challenge

**Place contradictions beside the claim**

Quote context, freshness, contract identity, neighboring legs, and missing fields remain part of the report.

## 6. 06 · Reproduce

**Make the report rerunnable**

Record the exact recipe, selected sessions, comparison fields, and resolution vocabulary for the next analyst.

## 7. 07 · Boundary

**Say what the report cannot say**

A small illustrative cohort describes process behavior; it does not establish probability, intent, direction, or universal performance.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Publish the challenge surface**

Next: S3E07 · run a blind replay before the next update and keep the prior resolution hidden.
