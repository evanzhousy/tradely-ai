# S3E06 storyboard

1. **01 · Question** — Open with the question, not the score — State what the repaired cohort was meant to test and what evidence could still lower the claim. — visual: `report-cover`
2. **02 · Scope** — Put the repair beside the result — Show symbol, contract, session range, recipe version, source lens, and as-of rules before the rows. — visual: `scope`
3. **03 · Rows** — Show every row in the cohort — Keep retained, lowered, unresolved, and missing rows visible so selection cannot disappear into a summary. — visual: `report-cohort`
4. **04 · Lineage** — Trace the repair boundary — Link each row to the method version that produced it; keep the old cohort historical and unchanged. — visual: `compound-card`
5. **05 · Challenge** — Place contradictions beside the claim — Quote context, freshness, contract identity, neighboring legs, and missing fields remain part of the report. — visual: `report-contradictions`
6. **06 · Reproduce** — Make the report rerunnable — Record the exact recipe, selected sessions, comparison fields, and resolution vocabulary for the next analyst. — visual: `report-reproduce`
7. **07 · Boundary** — Say what the report cannot say — A small illustrative cohort describes process behavior; it does not establish probability, intent, direction, or universal performance. — visual: `season-boundary`
8. **08 · Practice** — Publish the challenge surface — Next: S3E07 · run a blind replay before the next update and keep the prior resolution hidden. — visual: `s3e06-outro`
