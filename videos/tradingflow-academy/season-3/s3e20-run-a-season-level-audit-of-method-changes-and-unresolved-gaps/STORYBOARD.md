# S3E20 storyboard

1. **01 · Inventory** — List every method change — Name each repair question, changed field, recipe version, cohort window, and owner across the season. — visual: `s3e20-inventory`
2. **02 · Lineage** — Map the version history — Trace prior and current cohorts without backfilling a later repair into an earlier published read. — visual: `s3e20-lineage`
3. **03 · Scope** — Compare declared boundaries — Check that eligibility, freshness, required fields, and invalidations stayed explicit at every handoff. — visual: `s3e20-scope`
4. **04 · Gaps** — Aggregate unresolved inventory — Keep missingness, stale context, limited rows, and unresolved checks visible instead of averaging them away. — visual: `s3e20-gaps`
5. **05 · Review** — Challenge the audit — Ask another analyst to rerun the packet and locate the first field where the audit cannot support a claim. — visual: `s3e20-review`
6. **06 · Decision** — State what the season earned — Record retained, changed, retired, unresolved, and missing as process states—not a performance score. — visual: `s3e20-decision`
7. **07 · Handoff** — Freeze the inherited boundary — Publish the fields, ownership, invalidations, and history that the next season must carry forward. — visual: `s3e20-handoff`
8. **08 · Practice** — Close Season 3 — Next: S4E01 · start a new question with the audited boundary and keep this season’s history available. — visual: `s3e20-outro`
