# S3E20 narration outline

Run a Season-Level Audit of Method Changes and Unresolved Gaps

## 1. 01 · Inventory

**List every method change**

Name each repair question, changed field, recipe version, cohort window, and owner across the season.

## 2. 02 · Lineage

**Map the version history**

Trace prior and current cohorts without backfilling a later repair into an earlier published read.

## 3. 03 · Scope

**Compare declared boundaries**

Check that eligibility, freshness, required fields, and invalidations stayed explicit at every handoff.

## 4. 04 · Gaps

**Aggregate unresolved inventory**

Keep missingness, stale context, limited rows, and unresolved checks visible instead of averaging them away.

## 5. 05 · Review

**Challenge the audit**

Ask another analyst to rerun the packet and locate the first field where the audit cannot support a claim.

## 6. 06 · Decision

**State what the season earned**

Record retained, changed, retired, unresolved, and missing as process states—not a performance score.

## 7. 07 · Handoff

**Freeze the inherited boundary**

Publish the fields, ownership, invalidations, and history that the next season must carry forward.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Close Season 3**

Next: S4E01 · start a new question with the audited boundary and keep this season’s history available.
