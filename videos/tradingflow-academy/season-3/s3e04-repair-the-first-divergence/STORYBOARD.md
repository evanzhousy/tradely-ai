# S3E04 storyboard

1. **01 · Freeze** — Keep both historical reads — Lock Analyst A and Analyst B notes before changing a field, rule, or resolution. — visual: `retro-freeze`
2. **02 · Branch** — Mark the first divergence — Find the earliest field where selection, scope, missingness, method, or interpretation split. — visual: `retro-branch`
3. **03 · Cause** — Classify the divergence — Separate a missing data field from a method mismatch and from a legitimate interpretation gap. — visual: `retro-causes`
4. **04 · Repair** — Change one variable — Test one explicit field or rule while keeping the rest of the packet fixed. — visual: `retro-counterfactual`
5. **05 · Version** — Version the smallest repair — The old cohort stays historical; the repair belongs to a new method version. — visual: `retro-update`
6. **06 · Separate** — Keep cohorts comparable — Run the repaired rule on future rows only; never blend old and new recipe versions. — visual: `version-card`
7. **07 · Uncertainty** — Keep the remaining gap visible — A repair can narrow the disagreement without turning an unresolved state into a forecast. — visual: `uncertainty-card`
8. **08 · Practice** — Open the repaired replay — Next: S3E05 · run the repaired packet across a cohort and measure what actually changed. — visual: `s3e04-outro`
