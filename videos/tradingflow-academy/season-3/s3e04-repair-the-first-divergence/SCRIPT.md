# S3E04 narration outline

Repair the First Divergence

## 1. 01 · Freeze

**Keep both historical reads**

Lock Analyst A and Analyst B notes before changing a field, rule, or resolution.

## 2. 02 · Branch

**Mark the first divergence**

Find the earliest field where selection, scope, missingness, method, or interpretation split.

## 3. 03 · Cause

**Classify the divergence**

Separate a missing data field from a method mismatch and from a legitimate interpretation gap.

## 4. 04 · Repair

**Change one variable**

Test one explicit field or rule while keeping the rest of the packet fixed.

## 5. 05 · Version

**Version the smallest repair**

The old cohort stays historical; the repair belongs to a new method version.

## 6. 06 · Separate

**Keep cohorts comparable**

Run the repaired rule on future rows only; never blend old and new recipe versions.

## 7. 07 · Uncertainty

**Keep the remaining gap visible**

A repair can narrow the disagreement without turning an unresolved state into a forecast.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Open the repaired replay**

Next: S3E05 · run the repaired packet across a cohort and measure what actually changed.
