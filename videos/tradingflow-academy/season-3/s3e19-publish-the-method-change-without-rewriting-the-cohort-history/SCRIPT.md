# S3E19 narration outline

Publish the Method Change Without Rewriting the Cohort History

## 1. 01 · Record

**Write the method change**

Name the repair question, changed field, recipe version, and date before publishing a new interpretation.

## 2. 02 · Diff

**Show what changed**

Compare prior and current packet fields so another analyst can see the smallest change that moved forward.

## 3. 03 · History

**Keep the prior cohort intact**

Link the new method to future rows only; never replace a published read with a later version.

## 4. 04 · Scope

**Declare the next boundary**

State eligible rows, freshness, required fields, invalidations, and the exact cohort window before intake.

## 5. 05 · Evidence

**Attach the validation path**

Include blind review, rerun instructions, resolution vocabulary, and the owner who can challenge the packet.

## 6. 06 · Disclosure

**Publish the remaining gaps**

Keep missingness, stale context, unresolved checks, and limited rows visible beside the method change.

## 7. 07 · Ownership

**Sign the release**

Make reviewer, maintainer, replay operator, and escalation path accountable for the next cohort.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Open the season audit**

Next: S3E20 · run a season-level audit of method changes and unresolved gaps without turning process into performance.
