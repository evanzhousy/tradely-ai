# S3E12 narration outline

Publish the Update with Repair Lineage

## 1. 01 · Question

**Open with what changed**

State which repair the updated packet tested and what the new cohort can—and cannot—show.

## 2. 02 · Delta

**Show the row-level change**

Compare old and updated states by row without compressing a small illustrative sample into a score.

## 3. 03 · Lineage

**Connect the update to its origin**

Link the repair question, packet version, ownership, and future-row scope to every changed state.

## 4. 04 · History

**Keep the prior cohort visible**

Published history remains under its original rules; the update does not rewrite an earlier resolution.

## 5. 05 · Gaps

**Publish what remains unresolved**

Missing freshness, limited rows, stale context, and unresolved checks stay beside the update.

## 6. 06 · Challenge

**Make the update challengeable**

Record the exact recipe, fields, sessions, vocabulary, and invalidation another analyst should rerun.

## 7. 07 · Ownership

**Assign the next blind review**

Carry the remaining repair question and owner into a new boundary before opening the next cohort.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Open the next boundary**

Next: S3E13 · open the next blind review with the updated boundary and preserve this lineage.
