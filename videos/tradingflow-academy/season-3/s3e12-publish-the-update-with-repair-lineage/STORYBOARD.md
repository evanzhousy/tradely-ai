# S3E12 storyboard

1. **01 · Question** — Open with what changed — State which repair the updated packet tested and what the new cohort can—and cannot—show. — visual: `s3e12-review`
2. **02 · Delta** — Show the row-level change — Compare old and updated states by row without compressing a small illustrative sample into a score. — visual: `s3e12-delta`
3. **03 · Lineage** — Connect the update to its origin — Link the repair question, packet version, ownership, and future-row scope to every changed state. — visual: `compound-card`
4. **04 · History** — Keep the prior cohort visible — Published history remains under its original rules; the update does not rewrite an earlier resolution. — visual: `s3e12-history`
5. **05 · Gaps** — Publish what remains unresolved — Missing freshness, limited rows, stale context, and unresolved checks stay beside the update. — visual: `s3e10-gaps`
6. **06 · Challenge** — Make the update challengeable — Record the exact recipe, fields, sessions, vocabulary, and invalidation another analyst should rerun. — visual: `report-reproduce`
7. **07 · Ownership** — Assign the next blind review — Carry the remaining repair question and owner into a new boundary before opening the next cohort. — visual: `s3e12-next`
8. **08 · Practice** — Open the next boundary — Next: S3E13 · open the next blind review with the updated boundary and preserve this lineage. — visual: `s3e12-outro`
