# S3E01 narration outline

Test Whether Scope Prevents Drift

## 1. 01 · Handoff

**Keep Season 2 historical**

Carry the packet, cadence, and boundaries forward; do not rewrite the previous season to fit a new question.

## 2. 02 · Question

**State one process hypothesis**

Does a visible contract, session, and freshness packet make the first divergence easier to locate?

## 3. 03 · Scope

**Operationalize the terms**

Fix symbol, contract, session, source lens, required fields, and the checks that can lower the claim.

## 4. 04 · Baseline

**Run the baseline in TradingFlow**

Use Discover → Inspect → Validate → Compare and record the starting packet before adding a new framing.

## 5. 05 · Contrast

**Compare reads without changing the question**

Give analysts the same packet and compare where the evidence boundary splits—not who wins.

## 6. 06 · Alternatives

**Name what could explain a difference**

Missing fields, stale context, contract mismatch, and interpretation remain competing explanations.

## 7. 07 · Measure

**Measure the process claim**

Track first-divergence location, missingness, unresolved states, and rerunability—not a forecast score.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Open the Season 3 experiment**

Next: S3E02 · build the controlled evidence packet before comparing another read.
