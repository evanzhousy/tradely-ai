# S3E14 storyboard

1. **01 · Question** — Open with the blind question — State whether the freshness boundary made the first divergence easier to locate—not whether it predicted a direction. — visual: `s3e14-question`
2. **02 · Records** — Pair the sealed note and update — Bring the new blind note beside the prior update only after both records are locked. — visual: `blind-reveal`
3. **03 · Shared** — Align fields before resolutions — Compare symbol, contract, session, as-of line, freshness, quote context, and invalidation first. — visual: `s3e03-shared`
4. **04 · Result** — Name agreement and disagreement — Retained, lowered, unresolved, and missing describe the process result without a score or winner. — visual: `blind-log`
5. **05 · Cause** — Trace the first changed boundary — If the notes differ, locate selection, freshness, contract identity, or invalidation before changing the recipe. — visual: `blind-audit`
6. **06 · Repair** — Decide whether one field needs repair — A useful result may keep the packet unchanged, narrow one field, or open a new version for future rows. — visual: `s3e14-repair`
7. **07 · Publish** — Keep the result challengeable — Record the question, fields, rows, resolution, gaps, and ownership so another analyst can rerun the blind review. — visual: `report-reproduce`
8. **08 · Practice** — Open the next cohort decision — Next: S3E15 · decide whether the repair earns another cohort without rewriting this review history. — visual: `s3e14-outro`
