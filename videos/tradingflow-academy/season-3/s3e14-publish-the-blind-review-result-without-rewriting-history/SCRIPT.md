# S3E14 narration outline

Publish the Blind Review Result Without Rewriting History

## 1. 01 · Question

**Open with the blind question**

State whether the freshness boundary made the first divergence easier to locate—not whether it predicted a direction.

## 2. 02 · Records

**Pair the sealed note and update**

Bring the new blind note beside the prior update only after both records are locked.

## 3. 03 · Shared

**Align fields before resolutions**

Compare symbol, contract, session, as-of line, freshness, quote context, and invalidation first.

## 4. 04 · Result

**Name agreement and disagreement**

Retained, lowered, unresolved, and missing describe the process result without a score or winner.

## 5. 05 · Cause

**Trace the first changed boundary**

If the notes differ, locate selection, freshness, contract identity, or invalidation before changing the recipe.

## 6. 06 · Repair

**Decide whether one field needs repair**

A useful result may keep the packet unchanged, narrow one field, or open a new version for future rows.

## 7. 07 · Publish

**Keep the result challengeable**

Record the question, fields, rows, resolution, gaps, and ownership so another analyst can rerun the blind review.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Open the next cohort decision**

Next: S3E15 · decide whether the repair earns another cohort without rewriting this review history.
