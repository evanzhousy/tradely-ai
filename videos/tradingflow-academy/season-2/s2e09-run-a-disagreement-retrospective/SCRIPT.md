# S2E09 narration outline

Run a Disagreement Retrospective

## 1. 01 · Freeze

**Lock the original reads**

Keep Analyst A, Analyst B, and the cohort resolution verbatim before explaining why they differed.

## 2. 02 · Timeline

**Reconstruct the observed order**

Print, quote context, neighboring leg, and next snapshot stay in the order they were actually observed.

## 3. 03 · Branch

**Mark the first divergence**

Start at selection, missingness, scope, or interpretation—the earliest field where the evidence boundary split.

## 4. 04 · Causes

**Classify the cause before fixing it**

Data, method, interpretation, and boundary gaps need different repairs.

## 5. 05 · Counterfactual

**Choose the smallest repair**

Change one field or rule, test comparability, and keep the rest of the historical record fixed.

## 6. 06 · Update

**Version the repair for future rows**

Old cohort outcomes stay unchanged; the repaired method starts a new version for future reports.

## 7. 07 · Trail

**Leave an audit trail**

Keep original reads, divergence, repair, and remaining uncertainty together for the next review.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Make retrospectives compound**
