# S2E09 storyboard

1. **01 · Freeze** — Lock the original reads — Keep Analyst A, Analyst B, and the cohort resolution verbatim before explaining why they differed. — visual: `retro-freeze`
2. **02 · Timeline** — Reconstruct the observed order — Print, quote context, neighboring leg, and next snapshot stay in the order they were actually observed. — visual: `retro-timeline`
3. **03 · Branch** — Mark the first divergence — Start at selection, missingness, scope, or interpretation—the earliest field where the evidence boundary split. — visual: `retro-branch`
4. **04 · Causes** — Classify the cause before fixing it — Data, method, interpretation, and boundary gaps need different repairs. — visual: `retro-causes`
5. **05 · Counterfactual** — Choose the smallest repair — Change one field or rule, test comparability, and keep the rest of the historical record fixed. — visual: `retro-counterfactual`
6. **06 · Update** — Version the repair for future rows — Old cohort outcomes stay unchanged; the repaired method starts a new version for future reports. — visual: `retro-update`
7. **07 · Trail** — Leave an audit trail — Keep original reads, divergence, repair, and remaining uncertainty together for the next review. — visual: `retro-trail`
8. **08 · Practice** — Make retrospectives compound — Next: S2E10 · build a review cadence that opens the right packet at the right time. — visual: `s2e09-outro`
