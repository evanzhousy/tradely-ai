# S2E08 storyboard

1. **01 · Definitions** — Align words with facts — Observation, scope, persistence, and resolution are separate layers; define each before comparing reads. — visual: `definitions-card`
2. **02 · Rubric** — Use a shared resolution rubric — Retained, lowered, and unresolved describe how the evidence fits—not whether a trade was right. — visual: `rubric-card`
3. **03 · Compare** — Make the shared facts visible — Contract, scope, and time can match while the reading gap remains a legitimate question. — visual: `calibration-compare`
4. **04 · Uncertainty** — Keep unresolved as a valid state — When a check is incomplete, keep the next observation visible instead of forcing a yes or no. — visual: `uncertainty-card`
5. **05 · Version** — Version vocabulary changes — A new definition or check opens a new rubric version; old reports stay readable under the old one. — visual: `rubric-version`
6. **06 · Check** — Calibrate the boundary, not the outcome — Align which fields are required, what counts as missing, and which observation lowers the claim. — visual: `calibration-card`
7. **07 · Record** — Keep agreement and uncertainty together — Log matched facts, unresolved questions, and clarified definitions without deleting the disagreement. — visual: `agreement-log`
8. **08 · Practice** — Carry the calibrated rubric forward — Next: S2E09 · run a disagreement retrospective and repair the smallest method gap. — visual: `s2e08-outro`
