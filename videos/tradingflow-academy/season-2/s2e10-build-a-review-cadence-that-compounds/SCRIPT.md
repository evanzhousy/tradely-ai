# S2E10 narration outline

Build a Review Cadence That Compounds

## 1. 01 · Trigger

**Know when to open a packet**

New cohort, disagreement, missing field, or method change should create a visible review trigger.

## 2. 02 · Packet

**Use the same packet every time**

Original reads, scope, observed timeline, repair, version, and remaining uncertainty travel together.

## 3. 03 · Depth

**Match review depth to the problem**

A missing field needs a focused check; a method gap needs a full retrospective—not ceremony for its own sake.

## 4. 04 · Ownership

**Assign the repair and its status**

Make the checker, method owner, replay verifier, and open status visible until evidence lands.

## 5. 05 · Measure

**Measure process health**

Track missingness, reproducibility, unresolved states, and repair latency—not a prediction score.

## 6. 06 · Compound

**Version the smallest verified change**

Old cohorts stay historical; future rows use the repaired method version with a readable chain.

## 7. 07 · Carry

**Keep unresolved work visible**

A cadence closes the packet only when its boundary is explicit; open questions become the next trigger.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Make the review cadence compound**
