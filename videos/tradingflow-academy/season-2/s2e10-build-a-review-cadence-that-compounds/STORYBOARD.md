# S2E10 storyboard

1. **01 · Trigger** — Know when to open a packet — New cohort, disagreement, missing field, or method change should create a visible review trigger. — visual: `trigger-card`
2. **02 · Packet** — Use the same packet every time — Original reads, scope, observed timeline, repair, version, and remaining uncertainty travel together. — visual: `packet-card`
3. **03 · Depth** — Match review depth to the problem — A missing field needs a focused check; a method gap needs a full retrospective—not ceremony for its own sake. — visual: `levels-card`
4. **04 · Ownership** — Assign the repair and its status — Make the checker, method owner, replay verifier, and open status visible until evidence lands. — visual: `owner-card`
5. **05 · Measure** — Measure process health — Track missingness, reproducibility, unresolved states, and repair latency—not a prediction score. — visual: `measure-card`
6. **06 · Compound** — Version the smallest verified change — Old cohorts stay historical; future rows use the repaired method version with a readable chain. — visual: `compound-card`
7. **07 · Carry** — Keep unresolved work visible — A cadence closes the packet only when its boundary is explicit; open questions become the next trigger. — visual: `uncertainty-card`
8. **08 · Practice** — Make the review cadence compound — Next: S2E11 · publish a Season 2 review without turning process evidence into a scorecard. — visual: `s2e10-outro`
