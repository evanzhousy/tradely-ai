# S2E07 storyboard

1. **01 · Packet** — Give both analysts the same packet — Same contract, session, recipe version, source lens, and freshness line; no prior resolution is shown. — visual: `analyst-cover`
2. **02 · Reads** — Put the two resolutions side by side — Analyst A retains the claim; Analyst B lowers it because an alternative survives. — visual: `analyst-reads`
3. **03 · Facts** — Separate shared facts from interpretation — Timestamp and contract can match while persistence and resolution remain open to inspection. — visual: `facts-interpretation`
4. **04 · Divergence** — Find the first field that differs — Trace selection, missingness, scope, and interpretation before comparing final language. — visual: `divergence-card`
5. **05 · Checks** — Use shared checks to narrow the gap — Neighboring leg, OI persistence, follow-through, and invalidation should be the same checks for both reads. — visual: `evidence-bridge`
6. **06 · Record** — Keep nuance in the comparison log — Record matched facts, unresolved checks, and the field that needs clarification without forcing consensus. — visual: `agreement-log`
7. **07 · Calibrate** — Align boundaries, not conclusions — Shared vocabulary can improve comparability while preserving a legitimate unresolved state. — visual: `calibration-card`
8. **08 · Practice** — Make disagreement useful — Next: S2E08 · calibrate the read without collapsing uncertainty into yes or no. — visual: `s2e07-outro`
