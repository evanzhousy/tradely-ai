# S2E07 narration outline

Compare Analyst Reads Without Forcing Agreement

## 1. 01 · Packet

**Give both analysts the same packet**

Same contract, session, recipe version, source lens, and freshness line; no prior resolution is shown.

## 2. 02 · Reads

**Put the two resolutions side by side**

Analyst A retains the claim; Analyst B lowers it because an alternative survives.

## 3. 03 · Facts

**Separate shared facts from interpretation**

Timestamp and contract can match while persistence and resolution remain open to inspection.

## 4. 04 · Divergence

**Find the first field that differs**

Trace selection, missingness, scope, and interpretation before comparing final language.

## 5. 05 · Checks

**Use shared checks to narrow the gap**

Neighboring leg, OI persistence, follow-through, and invalidation should be the same checks for both reads.

## 6. 06 · Record

**Keep nuance in the comparison log**

Record matched facts, unresolved checks, and the field that needs clarification without forcing consensus.

## 7. 07 · Calibrate

**Align boundaries, not conclusions**

Shared vocabulary can improve comparability while preserving a legitimate unresolved state.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Make disagreement useful**

Next: S2E08 · calibrate the read without collapsing uncertainty into yes or no.
