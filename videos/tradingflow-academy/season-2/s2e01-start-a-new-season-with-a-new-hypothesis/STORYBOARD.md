# S2E01 storyboard

1. **01 · Reset** — Start with a question, not a conclusion — Season 2 carries the evidence discipline forward while leaving Season 1's claims historical. — visual: `season-next`
2. **02 · Packet** — Carry the review packet forward — Bring the scope, cadence, unresolved gaps, and version history into the next research pass. — visual: `packet-card`
3. **03 · Hypothesis** — Choose one testable question — Ask whether a repeated contract-level observation survives across sessions after scope and quote context are fixed. — visual: `hypothesis`
4. **04 · Scope** — Operationalize it before the data — Name the symbol, contract, session, filters, freshness line, and the observation that would lower the claim. — visual: `scope`
5. **05 · Baseline** — Run the first baseline in TradingFlow — Discover, inspect, validate, compare, and record the starting observation without turning it into a signal. — visual: `discover`
6. **06 · Alternatives** — Name the competing explanations — Hedge or spread, roll timing, stale structure, and missing follow-through stay in the test plan. — visual: `alternatives`
7. **07 · Measure** — Measure the method, not the outcome — Track replay coverage, disagreement, invalidation, and reproducibility; do not substitute a scorecard for evidence. — visual: `measure-card`
8. **08 · Practice** — Open Season 2 with a clean handoff — Next: S2E02 · turn the hypothesis into a replay recipe you can run without changing the question. — visual: `s2e01-outro`
