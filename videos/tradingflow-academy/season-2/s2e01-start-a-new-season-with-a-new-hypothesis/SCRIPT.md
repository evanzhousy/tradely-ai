# S2E01 narration outline

Start a New Season with a New Hypothesis

## 1. 01 · Reset

**Start with a question, not a conclusion**

Season 2 carries the evidence discipline forward while leaving Season 1's claims historical.

## 2. 02 · Packet

**Carry the review packet forward**

Bring the scope, cadence, unresolved gaps, and version history into the next research pass.

## 3. 03 · Hypothesis

**Choose one testable question**

Ask whether a repeated contract-level observation survives across sessions after scope and quote context are fixed.

## 4. 04 · Scope

**Operationalize it before the data**

Name the symbol, contract, session, filters, freshness line, and the observation that would lower the claim.

## 5. 05 · Baseline

**Run the first baseline in TradingFlow**

Discover, inspect, validate, compare, and record the starting observation without turning it into a signal.

## 6. 06 · Alternatives

**Name the competing explanations**

Hedge or spread, roll timing, stale structure, and missing follow-through stay in the test plan.

## 7. 07 · Measure

**Measure the method, not the outcome**

Track replay coverage, disagreement, invalidation, and reproducibility; do not substitute a scorecard for evidence.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Open Season 2 with a clean handoff**

Next: S2E02 · turn the hypothesis into a replay recipe you can run without changing the question.
