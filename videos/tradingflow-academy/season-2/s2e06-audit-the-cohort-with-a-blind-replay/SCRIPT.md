# S2E06 narration outline

Audit the Cohort with a Blind Replay

## 1. 01 · Blind start

**Hide the prior resolution**

Run the same question without showing whether the old cohort retained, lowered, or left the claim unresolved.

## 2. 02 · Fixed recipe

**Keep the inputs identical**

Same universe, expiry, moneyness, side, Vol/OI, source lens, and as-of rule.

## 3. 03 · Independent read

**Write a fresh observation**

Record contract, timestamp, side, size, and quote context before opening the old report.

## 4. 04 · Reveal

**Compare only after the note is locked**

Reveal the previous cohort resolution after the independent read—not before it.

## 5. 05 · Disagreement

**Treat disagreement as a diagnostic**

A different resolution can expose an ambiguous field or a method step that needs clarification.

## 6. 06 · Audit

**Trace the step that changed**

Check selection, missingness, scope, and invalidation—not which analyst supposedly won.

## 7. 07 · Record

**Version the audit result**

Log agreement, disagreement, and rule changes separately from the cohort outcome.

## 8. 08 · Practice

**Make blind replay routine**

Next: S2E07 · compare analyst reads without forcing agreement.
