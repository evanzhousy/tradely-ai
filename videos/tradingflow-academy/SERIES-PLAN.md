## Season arcs

### Season 1 — Evidence-first options research foundations (21)

Teach why a single print, wall, score, or chart is not a complete thesis. Establish the TradingFlow method: Discover → Inspect → Validate → Compare → Decide.

### Season 2 — Replayable research (11)

Turn a hypothesis into a replay recipe, run it across sessions and cohorts, compare analyst reads, preserve disagreement, and publish a review cadence.

### Season 3 — Controlled evidence and repair (20)

Make the research contract explicit: controlled packets, blind replay, repair lineage, future-row boundaries, ownership, method-change publication, and season-level audit. S3E20 hands off an audited boundary to Season 4.

### Season 4 — Product-native applied workflows (12)

Use the actual TradingFlow product as the teaching surface. Each episode connects one method rule to one inspectable workflow without turning a product ranking or metric into a signal.

## Definition of done for every episode

1. HyperFrames composition passes lint, runtime, layout, motion, and contrast checks.
2. Picture master is rendered at 1920×1080 with audio present.
3. Unified English subtitle rail is present, bottom-safe, readable, and does not cover the teaching surface.
4. Colorful TradingFlow logo appears on every frame.
5. A new-user subagent reviews the first-watch cut using specified keyframes plus mid-scene frames.
6. Required feedback is patched and the review is repeated; zero required changes are recorded before sign-off.
7. The canonical MP4, production status, and manifest row are updated.
