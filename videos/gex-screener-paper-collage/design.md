# GEX Paper Editorial

Physical layered editorial paper collage, photographed from directly above. Warm off-white uncoated paper field with visible fiber tooth. Cobalt TradingFlow blue is the focal accent; red-orange marks call-wall pressure, muted teal marks put-wall structure, and charcoal ink carries labels and halftone. Use fine round-dot halftone around 65 lpi, crisp machine-cut edges with slight handmade variance, thin warm-white keylines, and soft down-right shadows around 10px at roughly 24% opacity.

Every scene is a tactile pinball table or a folded paper map. The blue price puck is the recurring character. Use 8-10 visible elements per scene: a real textured field, the table or map, the puck, wall or regime pieces, 2-4 structural marks, and a short real-HTML label group. Keep the camera top-down and the lighting flat. No gradients, glossy 3D, neon, invented mascots, or recreated logos. The exact TradingFlow logo and product screenshots are composited from local assets.

Typography is real HTML for legibility: bold geometric sans for headings and labels, monospace for the URL and snapshot stamp, all-caps with moderate tracking. Product UI text comes from the exact screenshot assets and is never regenerated by the image model.
