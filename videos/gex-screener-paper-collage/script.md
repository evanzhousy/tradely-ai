# GEX Screener Paper-Collage Tutorial — Approval Draft

## Core dramatic action

An options trader wants to know why price sometimes sticks to a range and sometimes accelerates, but the dealer pressure is invisible; a paper pinball table reveals positive and negative gamma, then folds into TradingFlow's Rank Symbols map.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 · 0:00–0:07 · The strange table

**Picture:** On a warm paper field, a cobalt price puck taps a red-orange **CALL WALL** bumper twice while a teal **PUT WALL** catches the dips. On the third tap, the bumper tears open and the puck shoots off the table. A tiny paper trader watches, baffled.

**Narration:** “Price taps the same ceiling. Again. Again. Then one day, it flies through.”

**On-screen lesson:** `CALL WALL` · `PUT WALL`

### Scene 2 · 0:07–0:15 · Reveal the pressure

**Picture:** The same pinball table splits down the middle. On **POSITIVE GEX**, paper dealer hands push against the puck and compress a spring back toward the range. On **NEGATIVE GEX**, the hands chase the puck and stretch a paper arrow in the same direction. One visual joke: the positive side calmly catches the puck with a tiny cushion; the negative side needs a seat belt.

**Narration:** “The difference can be pressure you can't see.”

**On-screen lesson:** `POSITIVE GEX — hedge against the move` · `NEGATIVE GEX — hedge with the move`

### Scene 3 · 0:15–0:23 · Turn the table into a map

**Picture:** The table folds like a paper map and becomes a halftone version of the current Rank Symbols UI. Three numbered paper tabs land in sequence while the correct controls highlight: **1 Rank → Symbols**, **2 choose a GEX sort**, **3 open a ticker**.

**Narration:** “Start with the market's mood, not a prediction.”

**On-screen lesson:** `1  RANK → SYMBOLS` · `2  SORT BY GEX` · `3  OPEN A TICKER`

### Scene 4 · 0:23–0:32 · Read in order

**Picture:** The selected symbol drawer unfolds into four stacked paper cards: **GEX Environment**, **Zero Gamma**, **Call Wall**, **Put Wall**. A small blue spot marker slides across the map while a clearly separate paper stamp lands: **GEX STRUCTURE · LATEST SNAPSHOT**. A smaller label reads **SPOT · LIVE CONTEXT WHEN AVAILABLE**.

**Narration:** “Calm can mean mean reversion. Tension can mean acceleration. Either way, levels are possibilities—not promises.”

**On-screen lesson:** `READ: ENVIRONMENT → ZERO GAMMA → WALLS`

### Scene 5 · 0:32–0:38 · Complete the read

**Picture:** The gamma-regime card, wall map, and a paper option-flow tape click together into one compact dashboard. The price puck settles on the map. TradingFlow's exact logo appears with a one-beat paper click.

**Narration:** “Weather. Map. Then the tape. Explore GEX in TradingFlow.”

**On-screen CTA:** `EXPLORE GEX IN TRADINGFLOW` · `app.tradingflow.com/app/rank/symbols`
