## Video direction

- Palette system: pure white is the full-bleed ground; electric cobalt carries
  editorial type, rules, the permanent graph grid, and top/bottom hairlines.
  Subtitle type is black, and all other typography remains solid cobalt.
  Gradients are chart-only, with one monochromatic hue family per chart.
- Type system: Newsreader carries the dominant statement or figure; Hanken
  Grotesk carries descriptive copy; DM Mono carries dates, units, source tags,
  and page chrome.
- Motion grammar: smooth long-tail settles, one evidence reveal at a time, then
  enough stillness to read. No ambient loops.
- Editorial contract: every figure and thesis below comes from TradingFlow's
  published July 24 Daily Market Recap. GEX, DEX, and DEI are modeled estimates,
  not guarantees.
- Audio contract: Frederick narration is the anchor. A restrained electronic
  bed ducks beneath it, and 14 timed caption cards preserve the full story when
  playback begins muted.
- Brand contract: the official TradingFlow wordmark remains visible on every
  frame, centered above the scene content and preserving its blue-gradient
  symbol plus dark wordmark.
- Subtitle contract: every scene is cropped to a 900px visual stage while a
  separate 180px rail carries captions without overlapping the artwork.
- Entity-mark contract: SPX, SPY, QQQ, MU, SNDK, NVDA, TSLA, AAPL, META,
  MSFT, and AMZN receive compact official-color logo chips near their symbols.
- Ticker-label contract: whenever visible copy names a listed company, pair the
  name with its ticker in parentheses, for example `Microsoft (MSFT)`.
