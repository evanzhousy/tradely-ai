# Pink Dream Dollhouse

Reference-based three-storey dollhouse, modeled entirely in Blender before glTF export. The original reference's human child is excluded.

Includes bedroom, closet, bed, desk, chair, chandelier, living-room sofa and table, bathroom vanity and toilet, kitchen and refrigerator, dining set, toy scooter, pet accessories, plants, curved purple slide, pool, transparent guard panels, curved elevator cabin and exterior ladder.

Texture sources:

- Plastic micro-normal: ambientCG Plastic001, CC0, https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Plastic001
- Custom polka-dot / stripe / tile / quilting atlas: generated with ImageGen after searching ambientCG and Poly Haven for suitable reference-matching patterns. Applied in Blender and packed in the GLB.

Editable source: `artifacts/dollhouse/dollhouse.blend` (local).
Construction and revisions: `artifacts/dollhouse/build.py`, `finish.py`, `polish.py`.
Export: `artifacts/dollhouse/export.py`, executed through Blender MCP.

The geometry and materials are a stylized reference reconstruction, not a manufacturer CAD model. Tiny printed portraits are represented by decorative pattern panels.
